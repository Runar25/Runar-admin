HOW A READING WORKS

You come with a question — or without one. Both are welcome.

Before the stones speak, Rúnar asks a few things: your name, the area of life you are moving through, and what you are seeking. Not to judge. To listen more precisely.

Then you draw. One rune from twenty-five — the Blank among them, the rune of the unknown. What follows is one reading, in a single flow: what the rune carries for you today, told through one image.

The runes do not decide your path. They help you remember it.

RÚNAR'S VOICE

In the collection, every rune has Rúnar's own words about it — you can read them and listen to them in his voice. Recorded in advance. His teaching about the rune, not your reading.

Rúnar can also speak your own reading aloud. Not a recording. Each time different. Each time his.

He reads every one of your readings aloud. Afterwards his voice is kept with the reading in your journal, and you can listen again whenever you like. Without a subscription, he reads your free reading and the readings you pay for with a Rune Reading Card.

YOUR JOURNAL

Every reading you take for yourself is remembered. As a {seeker}, your last {seeker_journal} readings wait in the journal — the rune drawn, the date, the area, the words spoken.

When you move to {standard}, the journal becomes a full chronicle. Every reading, going back to the beginning. Filterable by rune, area, and language.

LANGUAGE

Rúnar speaks two languages — English and Icelandic. These are not translations of each other. Each carries its own imagery, its own rhythm. Icelandic is older here. The runes were carved first in that tongue.

You can switch languages at any time from the menu. Your preference is remembered.

YOUR ACCOUNT & PRIVACY

Sign in with a code sent to your email, or with Google — no password needed. Your readings are saved to your account.

Your data is stored in Europe (Ireland). Only our encrypted backups may be kept elsewhere. Iceland is a member of the European Economic Area. GDPR applies.

To delete your account and all your data, open the menu and choose Delete Account. Everything is erased at once, though our encrypted backups keep the data for a while longer. Read our full Privacy Policy.
