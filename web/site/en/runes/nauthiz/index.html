RÚNAR ISEN

NAUTHIZ

13 NOVEMBER – 27 NOVEMBER
The life rune of those born on these days.
✦ HOW RÚNAR SEES THE RUNE

An upright line with a short stroke laid across it at a slant, like a bar across a doorway. The line goes on below it, but not without having met it.

Nauthiz is the feeling that something is missing. Some doors are closed now, and wanting will not open them.

Before you look for a way out, it asks: what do you lack so badly now that you can no longer pretend you do not need it?
