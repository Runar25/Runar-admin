RÚNAR ISEN

PERTH

13 JANUARY – 27 JANUARY
The life rune of those born on these days.
✦ HOW RÚNAR SEES THE RUNE

An upright line with two bent strokes on its side, open like a cup tipped on its side, or a lid lifted just enough to guess what is inside. You see the opening, not what it holds.

Perth is a closed vessel. You can guess what it holds, but until it opens, part of the picture stays hidden.

One question remains: what in your life can you not yet name — and what if that is because it is not ready to open?
