RÚNAR ISEN

ALGIZ

28 JANUARY – 12 FEBRUARY
The life rune of those born on these days.
✦ HOW RÚNAR SEES THE RUNE

An upright line that opens into three at the top, like an elk lifting its antlers, or a raised hand. It is the shape of something that has heard a sound and does not yet know what it was.

Algiz is watchfulness that stays open. Like someone in a forest who hears something and lifts their head before knowing what it was.

Perhaps it asks you: what do you need to protect now — and what do you not need to hold your boundary against?

The full text is in the app, and Rúnar reads it to you.
