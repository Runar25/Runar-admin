RÚNAR ISEN

URUZ

14 JULY – 28 JULY
The life rune of those born on these days.
✦ HOW RÚNAR SEES THE RUNE

One line stands straight, and from its top a second comes down on a slant, like a broad back, or a head lowered to push. It stands on its own two legs and leans its weight forward.

Uruz is the strength of an aurochs, heavy and untamed. It asks no one whether it may pass.

Rather than telling you to be stronger, it asks: what strength is already in you, and what could come of it?
