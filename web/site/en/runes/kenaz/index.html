RÚNAR ISEN

KENAZ

13 SEPTEMBER – 27 SEPTEMBER
The life rune of those born on these days.
✦ HOW RÚNAR SEES THE RUNE

Two short lines meet in a point and open to the right, like light spreading from a single flame. It does not fill the room; it reaches only as far as the next step.

Kenaz is a light that reaches only a step ahead of you, to a door that was there all along, unseen.

Then it asks: what do you see more clearly than before — and what will you do with it, now that you cannot look past it?
