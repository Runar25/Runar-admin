RÚNAR ISEN

INGWAZ

14 MAY – 28 MAY
The life rune of those born on these days.
✦ HOW RÚNAR SEES THE RUNE

A small diamond whose sides run on past its top and bottom corners and cross there. Like a seed in its husk: closed in the middle, holding everything it needs and letting nothing out until it is ready.

Ingwaz is a seed in the earth: no longer what it was, not yet a plant.

Perhaps what to begin matters less now. Ask instead: what in you has ripened enough that you no longer need to hold it inside?
