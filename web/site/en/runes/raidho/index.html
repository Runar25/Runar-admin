RÚNAR ISEN

RAIDHO

29 AUGUST – 12 SEPTEMBER
The life rune of those born on these days.
✦ HOW RÚNAR SEES THE RUNE

An upright line with a loop at its top and a leg stepping out below, forward and to the right. It is a shape in motion: something leaning into the road, one foot already on its way.

Raidho is a road already begun. Step follows step, and only when you look back do you see the road they made.

Perhaps Raidho asks: does your road truly lead where you want to go — or do you walk on only because you once set out?
