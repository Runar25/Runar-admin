RÚNAR ISEN

FEHU

29 JUNE – 13 JULY
The life rune of those born on these days.
✦ HOW RÚNAR SEES THE RUNE

One upright line with two short arms lifted from it to the right. You can see the horns of a beast in it, turned to the side, or a stem that has put out shoots and is still growing. Either way it reaches outward; it does not stay still.

Fehu is value that moves. Like water poured into a vessel: for a while there is too little, then it runs over.

It may ask you: what is in your hands now that could come alive if you let it move?

The full text is in the app, and Rúnar reads it to you.
