RÚNAR ISEN

JERA

13 DECEMBER – 27 DECEMBER
The life rune of those born on these days.
✦ HOW RÚNAR SEES THE RUNE

Two small bent strokes, one above and one below, each turned toward the other without touching. Like two halves of the year going round, one bringing in what the other sowed.

Jera is time at work. A seed lies long unseen in the ground, until the first shoot appears.

Perhaps Jera asks: what are you harvesting in your life now — and what did you sow long before you knew what that harvest would look like?
