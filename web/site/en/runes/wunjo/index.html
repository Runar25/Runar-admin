RÚNAR ISEN

WUNJO

13 OCTOBER – 27 OCTOBER
The life rune of those born on these days.
✦ HOW RÚNAR SEES THE RUNE

An upright line with a small closed triangle at its top, like a flag raised over a house when everyone has come home. The shape is complete; nothing in it is still reaching.

Wunjo is the moment something falls into place. It is no great victory; for a while you can loosen your grip.

It does not ask how to keep it. It asks: where in your life is there already something good — and will you let yourself fully enjoy it?
