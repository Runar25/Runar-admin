HAGALAZ

28 OCTOBER – 12 NOVEMBER
The life rune of those born on these days.
✦ HOW RÚNAR SEES THE RUNE

Two upright lines, and a single stroke falling across the gap between them at a slant, like hail driving between two posts. The posts still stand when it has passed.

Hagalaz is hail that falls without warning. In a few moments it changes what looked solid.

So the question is not how to stop it, but: what is falling in your life now — and what will remain when the storm has passed?
