RÚNAR ISEN

LAGUZ

29 APRIL – 13 MAY
The life rune of those born on these days.
✦ HOW RÚNAR SEES THE RUNE

An upright line with a short stroke falling from its top to one side, like a wave breaking, or water running off a ledge. It bends downward, the way water always finds the lower way.

Laguz is water. It can mirror the sky, yet shows nothing of its own.

Perhaps it asks: what do you feel inside before you have words for it?

The full text is in the app, and Rúnar reads it to you.
