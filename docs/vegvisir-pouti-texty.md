## POUŤ 1

domov: balvanitá suť (Uruz) · runy Fehu→Isa→Ansuz→Perth→Wunjo→Nauthiz→Dagaz→Mannaz  
trasa: domov → lávová pláň → jeskyně → bažina → hverasvæði → čelo ledovce → říční náplavy → štěrková pláň

**1 · Fehu** — *domov*

> Water seeps from under the great fallen stones, threading down through moss and fog into the scree below. Fehu is what a spring gives simply by running, Anna, one steady thread from the whole hidden weight of stone. You stand where that thin water finds its way out.

**2 · Isa** — *lávová pláň*

> A thin crack runs through the black lava, dry now where the fog once beaded and hung. Isa is the pause that holds a thing still until its moment. The moss waits in the deeper hollows, green and patient, going nowhere in this hard sun.

**3 · Ansuz** — *jeskyně*

> Daylight thins at the cave mouth, where the last of it catches the wet roof and the drops falling from it. Ansuz is the way a word passes from the lit air into the dark and is heard there, Anna. Behind you the lava has hardened black and still, ahead the drops keep their slow speaking. You stand at the mouth where the light stops.

**4 · Perth** — *bažina*

> Under the black pools something waits that the surface will not show, held in dark water that gives back only sky. Perth is what the mire keeps until it is ready to release it. The rain that ran off the roof behind you now sits soaking in this ground, going nowhere.

**5 · Wunjo** — *hverasvæði*

> Yellow sulphur crusts ring the simmering pits, brittle where the vapour breaks through after the rain, and one pit holds still while the others seethe. That still one draws you closer. Wunjo is the ground where you find you are not standing alone. Do you kneel at the quiet rim, or walk on toward the roar?

**6 · Nauthiz** — *čelo ledovce*

> The ice gives up its stones now, working them loose into the grey sand as the meltwater runs hard beneath it. Nauthiz is the long grinding that shaped each one, Anna, the pressure that made the edge. No sulphur stains this ground; here the rock came clean through the cold. Every stone at your feet was carried by force it did not choose.

**7 · Dagaz** — *říční náplavy*

> The gravel bars hold their line, Anna, while the braided channels shift across them in the low sun, the grey of yesterday's sand now washed pale and glittering here. Dagaz is that first light finding water that moved all night. You are standing on the dry bar between the channels.

**8 · Mannaz** — *štěrková pláň*

> Across the wet gravel plain the stones lie scattered, each one dark and alone, the old bars long since broken apart. Then one stone holds your eye, and the rest fall from your seeing. This is Mannaz, the way a single self stands clear against everything wider around it.

## POUŤ 2

domov: fjara u ústí řeky (Laguz) · runy Thurisaz→Berkana→Raidho→Othila→Eihwaz→Gebo→Hagalaz→Tiwaz  
trasa: domov → lávová pláň → jeskyně → rokle → stráň → údolí → drnová stavení → oblázkový val

**1 · Thurisaz** — *domov*

> The thin line of foam edges over the hard black sand, one reach at a time. This, Anna, is Thurisaz — the sea taking back the ground the low tide lent, quiet and without asking. What was firm underfoot goes under while you stand there watching.

**2 · Berkana** — *lávová pláň*

> Deep in the cracks of this broken lava, Anna, thin green threads of moss push up through the black, where the sand ground down years ago and settled into every hollow. Berkana is that first living root taking hold in what looked like bare stone. The sun beats hard on the field, yet the green stays low and holds. Something small has already begun in the dark seam.

**3 · Raidho** — *jeskyně*

> The daylight holds steady at the cave mouth while the dark deepens behind you and drops fall from the black roof. Raidho is the pace a walk keeps without being told, the moss now gone from this bare wet stone. You stand at the lit edge, where the dripping begins.

**4 · Othila** — *rokle*

> Down in the ravine, Anna, moss covers the wet stones green while fog drifts and the stream murmurs below. Look closer and only the moss remains, holding to rock it did not choose. This is Othila, the old growth loosening its grip so new can root. What stays green here, and what has already thinned?

**5 · Eihwaz** — *stráň*

> Where the grass gives way to bare stone, a thin trickle of water finds its line down the slope. Eihwaz is the way water holds its course through hard and soft alike, wearing a channel where the moss has not yet reached. It does not choose the easy ground, only the ground that lets it keep moving. Does the water bend around the stone, or does it wait for the stone to give?

**6 · Gebo** — *údolí*

> The fences stand across the grass while the fog gives way around them, drifting off the valley floor toward the river. Gebo is the give and take between two posts that a single wire needs to hold. The water that ran thin behind you gathers here, level and quiet in the grass.

**7 · Hagalaz** — *drnová stavení*

> Behind the stone walls between the turf houses, the frost of old winters has worked loose one stone, and the gap waits under the evening light. Hagalaz is the crack that opens where the wall seemed whole. What did the leaning stones let through before anyone looked?

**8 · Tiwaz** — *oblázkový val*

> Where the sea gave up this driftwood, the grain runs straight though the bark is long gone, worn pale on the dark bank of rolled stones. Tiwaz is that grain holding true after the water took everything softer away. What in you, Anna, stays straight when the surface is stripped off?
