The raven rides the wind high over the fells, working the currents that are moving right now, not the ones that passed. Fehu is that living air beneath the wings — energy already in motion, ready to be spent or held. What you have gathered is meant to move.
Uruz gives you the strength that turns cut grass into winter fodder, raw power made useful by the long light. It offers force, and asks in return that you point it somewhere. Where would you set this weight down if you finally trusted your own arms?
Thurisaz is the thorn that guards the root, and it asks something before it gives: that you stop reaching where the summer rain drives sideways across the lava. Not every hand held out should be taken. Some doors keep more than they keep out.
Ansuz is the word that carries across the fjord in the pale light that never fully sets, and reaches the far shore whole. It gives you the breath to speak plainly, and asks only that you mean what you send. What have you left unsaid because the timing never came?
Raidho gives you the road that is already under your feet, and asks only that you keep the pace that is yours. The basalt columns stand in even ranks, each one rising in its own time. Move as they were made, and the way carries you.
Kenaz is the low midnight sun laying its gold across the water, the one light still burning while the world goes quiet. It gives you sight in this hour, and asks only that you make something of what you see. The making is yours to do.
Gebo moves like the tide against these skerries — the sea gives to the rock, the rock gives back its shape, neither poorer for it. What you hold out now returns by a different hand. You stand on even ground, both palms open.
You stand where the path splits and the grass is high on both sides. Wunjo is the sheep already grazing on the green slope above, needing nothing more than the light of this hour. The joy you are waiting for is already underfoot.
Hagalaz falls now, in the season when the rain will not clear and the roots drink more than they can hold. Something under you is loosening in the wet, quietly, before you have named it. What are you still pretending is solid?
Nauthiz sits low in your gut, a tightness that will not loosen no matter how you turn. It is the pull of the root that grips the bare grey gravel where nothing else holds. What are you carrying that you have never once set down?
Isa is the water that stopped moving, held fast below the frost line where the roots still reach down. You stand between staying and going, the north wind cutting through this light that will not dim. What are you refusing to admit has already frozen solid?
The crowberries ripen dark on the lava, warm under a sun that took all summer to reach them — this is Jera, the slow turn come round at last. What waited in you has done its waiting. What are you still holding back that is already ready to be gathered?
Eihwaz holds by what it hides — the yew's roots grip deep in dark soil, taking the weight no one sees. This asks a stubbornness that costs you, an endurance paid in silence. The angelica stands green by the stream, and so do you, still holding.
Perth sits low in your gut, that held breath before you turn a stone you have left unturned for years. Sun warms the green turf roof while the dark under the sod stays cool and untouched. What are you keeping in that shadow because naming it would cost you something?
The tall grass leans toward the open sky above the meadow, roots holding the earth while the tips reach into moving air. This is Algiz — the reaching that stays anchored. What are you reaching toward that you have not yet named aloud?
Sowilo is the light that finds the water first, warming the fjord before the day has fully woken. Its gift is clarity, and it asks only that you stay in the open where it can reach you. The whales surface into that stillness, and the light holds them.
Tiwaz gives you the straight line that holds when everything around it bends, and asks that you spend yourself for it. The glacial river runs milky over black sand, choosing one channel and giving up all the others. Which fight are you carrying that is no longer yours to win?
Berkana is the first green pushing through the highland now the passes have opened, small and stubborn against the bare ground. It does not ask permission to grow, it simply begins where the light finds it. What is already starting in you before you have named it?
Ehwaz gives you a horse already at a trot, the reins loose in your hand across a slope where wild thyme runs pink and low in the sun. What it asks is that you let it carry its share, not grip the whole weight yourself. Move together, and the ground goes easy under you.
The waterfall falls white and full in the midday light, yet the river is many waters gathered from many slopes. Mannaz is the meeting of all you have carried and all who carried you. You stand where your own mind widens to hold others.
Laguz moves under the valley floor, water the roots drink long before the surface knows it. A rainbow stands over the wet grass after the shower, one foot in ground you cannot see. What is already fed in you, waiting to show?
Ingwaz holds what the puffin holds in its beak, crossing the whole grey sea to bring one small silver fish back to the burrow. The gift is what waits, gathered and still. What it asks is that you carry it the full distance before you set it down. You are still over open water.
The cotton-grass nods white across the bog, rooted in ground that older feet than yours once crossed. Othila is the field that was tended before you and reaches now toward the wider sky. What was handed to you still holds beneath your weight.
Dagaz is the light that comes only after the whole night has been spent, and it asks for that night first. The lupine did not open the hillside in one morning; it took every cold dawn it was given. What breaks open in you will cost you the waiting.
You stand where the path has not yet been laid, the ground beneath still soft and unnamed. The blank rune is the cotton-grass swaying white over the bog, rooted in water no eye can see. What have you not yet let yourself want?
