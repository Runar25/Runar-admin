## 2. A — deset čtení
- Obrazy: 7 prošlých + 3 hraniční z kroku 45, přesný čas odstraněn („for weeks“ → pryč, „a year ago“ → „long ago“,
  „ten pages“ → „enough pages“, „for an hour“ → „for a long while“, „mile after mile“ → „on and on“).
- Pokyn (šablona níž): věta 1 = co runa znamená, vlastními slovy, jméno smí stát kdekoli (bez „here“ —
  krok 44 ho dělal pokyn); věta 2 = obraz v poloze z losu (A uvnitř / B pozoruje / C bez Imagine); věta 3 = stín významu bez
  „The danger is“; věta 4 = otázka **z významu runy, ne z detailu obrazu**; zákaz přesného času v pokynu.
- Dva pisatelé (čerství agenti), každý 5 čtení.

```
RUNE: {rune}
WHAT THIS READING SHOWS OF {rune} (one side of its meaning; say it in your own words, do not copy it): {area}
THE IMAGE (a scene that carries that meaning; tell it in your own words, keep what happens in it): {img}
Say nothing as fact about the reader's own life: no named family roles (...), no past or feeling of the reader stated as fact. (...)
No exact span of time or date anywhere (no "for weeks", "a year ago", "last summer"); if time matters, keep it open ("for a while", "long after").

THE READING: 4 sentences, 50 to 60 words, in this order:
1: what {rune} means, said plainly in your own words; the name of the rune may stand anywhere in the sentence.
2: the image. {pozice}
3: the shadow of this meaning: what can go wrong inside it, not a fault of the reader.
4: one open question that grows from the meaning of {rune}, not from the details of the image, and that different readers could take in different ways.
Reply with the reading only.
```

### Čtení (doslova)
1. **F3s** (Fehu · co se schovává, ztrácí cenu · B) — FEHU is wealth that lives only while it is used; kept back, it slowly loses its worth. Imagine a woman opening her wardrobe: the best coat still hangs with its tags on, and the moths found it first. The shadow is caution dressed as care, guarding a thing until it is worth nothing. What are you saving, and for whom?
2. **F3n** (Fehu · odříznuté od zdroje · C) — Fehu is wealth that stays alive only while it flows, and what is cut off from its source loses its strength. A pool in the lava field, no longer fed by its spring, turns green and still and sinks slowly into the moss. Nothing breaks; the water simply stays until it is gone. What still feeds what you hold?
3. **G1s** (Gebo · dar, který spojí · A) — GEBO is the gift that ties a thread between people where none ran before. Imagine you clear the snow from your own doorstep and from the silent neighbour's too, and from then on they nod to you. Its shadow is a gift with a hidden hook, which binds instead of joining. What bond is waiting for a first gift?
4. **G2s** (Gebo · dar s dluhem · B) — Gebo is the gift that binds two people, and sometimes the binding grows heavier than the gift itself. Imagine someone keeping a borrowed drill by the door until there is something good enough to return with it. The kindness becomes a weight, and the longer it waits, the harder returning gets. When does a gift stop being free?
5. **G2n** (Gebo · dávat a brát se drží · A) — GEBO is an exchange where giving and receiving hold each other up. Imagine you leave scraps on your farm step for a raven, and it brings bright pebbles back, until the scraps stop and so does the raven. The shadow is a bond that lasts only as long as the trade keeps balance. Which side do you hold up?
6. **R1s** (Raidho · smysl až zpětně · C) — Raidho is a road whose sense shows only when you look back along it. Someone opens a textbook in a new language and the first page means nothing, yet after enough pages the first one reads clearly. Midway, the road can feel pointless, and it is easy to stop before it speaks. Which steps make sense only later?
7. **R2s** (Raidho · vlastní tempo · B) — RAIDHO is the road travelled at your own pace, even when others walk it too. Imagine a long hike where the fast walkers stand cold at every turn, waiting, while the slow one at the back never once has to stop. The shadow comes when everyone keeps another's speed and no one keeps their own. Whose pace are you walking?
8. **R3s** (Raidho · způsob cesty neodpovídá cíli · A) — Raidho is the way you travel, and sometimes the way you travel no longer fits where you are headed. Imagine you drive the old road to a job left long ago and notice only at the last junction. Habit steers so smoothly that the wrong road feels like the right one. Which way of moving still belongs to you?
9. **R3n** (Raidho · ztráta směru za chůze · C) — RAIDHO is direction on the road, and it can be lost even while you keep walking. In a snowstorm on the heath, a walker follows fresh tracks for a long while before seeing that they are her own. The shadow is movement mistaken for progress, a circle that feels like a path. How would you know you were circling?
10. **R2n** (Raidho · vlastní rytmus · A) — Raidho is a steady rhythm of your own that carries you far. Imagine you are riding across the black sand in a smooth running walk, barely moving in the saddle, the same four beats on and on. So easy a rhythm can become drifting, the beat going on after the direction is lost. What pace is truly yours?
