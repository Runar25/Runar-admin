## Texty
- R0-1: The sea lays shells upon the shore and draws the sand back out, all in one breath. Gebo asks you to see your giving and receiving as that same tide. Offer freely, yet let yourself be given to without shame or tally. Where both hands stay open, a true bond forms, balanced as water and stone.
- R0-2: The tide does not ask whether it is giving or taking; it moves, and the shore is shaped by both. Gebo comes to you as that same breath, an exchange between equals. Let yourself receive as freely as you give, for a gift refused breaks the circle. Whatever you offer now returns, changed and salted, on the next wave.
- R0-3: The tide lays shells at your feet and draws the sand back from beneath them in the same breath, and Gebo calls this no loss. What you give returns to you shaped by other hands. Let a bond in your life find its even exchange, neither hoarded nor poured out. Give freely, receive without shame, and the shore holds.
- R1-1: The sea gives to the shore and takes from it in the same breath, and neither is poorer for it. Gebo asks you to look at what passes between you and those near you. Where you only give, or only receive, something has grown heavy. Let the tide move both ways, and the bond will hold.
- R1-2: The sea does not hold back its giving, nor apologise for what it draws away; both are one motion. Gebo asks you to look at where you give and where you receive, and whether either has grown heavy. A true gift binds without chaining. Let the tide move both ways, and the shore stays whole.
- R1-3: The sea gives to the shore and takes from it in one breath, and neither keeps score. Gebo asks you to look at what moves between you and another. A gift held too tightly stops being a gift, and so does one given only to be owed. Let the tide go both ways, and see what stays.
