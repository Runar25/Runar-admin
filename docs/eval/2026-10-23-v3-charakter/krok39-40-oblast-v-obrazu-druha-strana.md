## 4. Čtení — krok 39 (druhá strana uvnitř, věta 3)

1. **Fehu · Family · protiklad · humor** Wealth lives only while it moves, like the casserole dish passed from aunt to aunt, always returned full, while the good china sits behind glass for funerals. The dish is worth more each time it leaves the house. But a dish that never comes back becomes a debt, and families keep count. Whose kitchen is it in now?
2. **Fehu · Healing · zákonitost** What is fed grows and what is left standing weakens, as a small tear mended early holds, while one ignored runs wider with each wearing. The needle does little; what matters is whether anyone picks it up. Yet cloth worked every day also frays, and some garments mend best folded away. Which piece will you reach for next?
3. **Fehu · Love · tělo** Fehu is value given as work, attention, a bond or something handmade, like passing a warm cup across a table, the heat leaving your palm as it reaches theirs. That warmth is real wealth, but it lives only while the cup keeps moving. Some hands only take, and yours go cold reaching. Whose cup came back warm?
4. **Uruz · Love · vlastní obraz** Uruz is strength that comes when it is needed, not shown off, like an aurochs grazing quietly beside a friend until someone comes too close. It does not bellow for an audience; it plants its feet when the ground gives. But a beast that waits unseen goes unthanked for years. What lies quiet in you right now, waiting?
5. **Uruz · Inner Growth · děj · humor** Uruz is a time that forces change: the old no longer fits and the new has no shape yet, like a hermit crab wearing a shell it outgrew last spring. The pinch grows daily, and pretending never made a shell roomier. Yet between shells the crab is soft, and soft things grow. What will you crawl toward?
6. **Uruz · Unseen · situace** Uruz is the power to change, not a ready answer: a man at the dinner table, jaw tight, holding back the old words. The strength is in the holding, which gives him room to choose differently. Held too long, that force turns into a silence that sets like cold fat in a pan. What did he finally say?
7. **Gebo · Crossroads · stav** A gift is only a gift if it can be refused, like a plate held out across the table while the other's hands still rest in their lap. Everything sits in the gap between plate and hands. Push the plate closer and the bread stays the same, yet it weighs like a debt. What keeps those hands still?
8. **Gebo · Healing · protiklad** Gebo is a gift between two sides that makes something new, like a quilt two people stitch at one table, not a blanket bought finished. Each square given asks for one back, and the seam binds both. That seam is a debt too: if one stops, the other holds half a quilt. Whose square goes down next?
9. **Gebo · Inner Growth · zákonitost · humor** Taking a gift before you know its use is like planting an acorn: it never says whether you'll get shade or just fatter squirrels. You take it anyway, and the use shows itself ring by slow ring. But some lie buried for years, and an unused gift becomes mere weight. What have you taken that is still underground?
10. **Isa · Family · tělo** Isa is a movement that stops with nowhere to hurry, like a family at the kitchen table after the phone call, hands flat on the wood. No one stands to clear the plates, since rushing would change nothing. Yet in that quiet, people who rarely sit together are shoulder to shoulder. Whose hand rests nearest yours?
11. **Isa · Unseen · vlastní obraz** Some things will not be hurried; a pond freezes over and looks finished, yet under the ice the water still moves. What you see is only the top; the current keeps its own pace. But water shut under ice too long goes stale and dark. When the thaw comes, will you break the ice or wait for it?
12. **Isa · Crossroads · děj** Isa is not an end but a moment before its time: a sealed letter on the table, the stamp waiting beside it. Look at the sealed envelope: the words inside are finished, only the sending waits. But a letter left too long yellows, and the waiting answers for you. What have you left lying on a table?

## 5. Čtení — krok 40 (4 věty + druhá strana zvlášť)

1. **Fehu · Family · humor** Fehu is wealth alive only while it moves, like the casserole dish travelling from aunt to cousin and back, against the good china behind glass, dusted for funerals. Each kitchen fills it and adds a name on tape underneath. But a travelling dish gets chipped, and nobody remembers whose it was. Whose dish is in your kitchen now?
   *Druhá strana:* Hold it still to keep it safe and it turns into the china, kept forever and never eaten from.
2. **Fehu · Healing** What you feed grows and what you leave standing weakens, as a leg kept long in a cast comes out thin. Rest mends the bone, but only walking puts the muscle back. Walk too soon, though, and the break opens again. Which part needs the cast a while longer, and which is ready to bear weight?
   *Druhá strana:* Yet the thin leg is not lost; fed with steps, it remembers how to carry you.
3. **Fehu · Love** Wealth can be work or care, like the warmth in your hands as you pass a friend a loaf you baked. The value lies in the hours inside it, and the other person feels their weight. But warmth fades, and a loaf given too often gets eaten without a glance. Who stopped looking up from the table?
   *Druhá strana:* The same warm loaf can turn into a debt, pressed into hands that never asked to be fed.
4. **Uruz · Love** Uruz is strength that rises when needed, not when watched, like the quiet aurochs in a friend that lowers its horns only when someone comes at you. The rest of the time it grazes, and no one would guess its weight. But what waits that long gets taken for tame. What grazes in you now, unseen?
   *Druhá strana:* The same beast can graze so long that, when the moment finally comes, it has forgotten how to stand.
5. **Uruz · Inner Growth · humor** Uruz is when the old no longer fits and the new has no shape yet, like a hermit crab slowly outgrowing its shell until it is mostly wearing a hat. The push comes from inside, and the shell cannot argue. The cost is the soft walk between shells. Take the first shell, or wait for one that fits?
   *Druhá strana:* Only a crab that leaves the tight shell ever finds one with room to spare.
6. **Uruz · Unseen** Uruz is the strength to change, not a ready answer: a man's first morning without tobacco, his hand still reaching for the empty pocket. The strength is pulling the hand back each time, with nothing yet to fill it. By evening he is tired, and it bought him one day. What was that hand really reaching for?
   *Druhá strana:* The same stubborn hand that learned the old habit is the one now learning to stay still.
7. **Gebo · Crossroads** A gift is only a gift when it can be refused, like a bowl of plums held out across a fence, the neighbor's hands still on the gate. Everything lives in the gap between the bowl and her hands. Push it into her arms and the plums arrive, but the gift does not cross. What rests on the gate now?
   *Druhá strana:* A bowl held out too long, too politely, goes soft in the sun while she still decides.
8. **Gebo · Healing** Gebo is a gift that binds two sides, like torn edges stitched into one seam, not a patch laid loose on top. Each thread pulls on both edges, so the seam holds only while both give. But stitches pucker where one side pulls harder, and a seam can't be undone without tearing. Which edge will you offer?
   *Druhá strana:* The same seam that holds you together also holds you in place.
9. **Gebo · Inner Growth · humor** Taking a gift before knowing its use works like a cutting on the windowsill, which grows roots whether or not anyone has bought a pot. Accepting was the whole agreement; deciding comes later. Leave it too long, though, and the glass gets crowded and the roots decide for you. What did you once leave standing in water?
   *Druhá strana:* Yet some cuttings live for years in the glass, never potted, and stay quietly green there.
10. **Isa · Family** Isa is a stop with nowhere to hurry, like a family at the kitchen table after the news, hands flat on the wood. Nobody clears the cups, and nobody needs to yet. Sit too long and the tea goes cold, and the youngest begins to fidget. Who is still sitting at that table now?
    *Druhá strana:* In some kitchens nobody stands for years, because no one remembers who is supposed to move first.
11. **Isa · Unseen · vlastní obraz** Some things cannot be hurried; when a river freezes, the water keeps running underneath, out of sight. The still white top is not the whole river; below, the current still carries things where they were going. But in the shallows the ice reaches the bottom, and nothing moves. Will you wait for the thaw, or cut a hole?
    *Druhá strana:* The fish live through the winter under the ice, because that cold lid keeps the deep water from freezing.
12. **Isa · Crossroads** Isa is no end but the hour before its time, like a pen held over an unsigned page, ink gathering at the nib. In that pause, one line comes clear: the exact words your name would stand under. Wait too long and the drop falls anyway, a blot where the signature belongs. What did that waiting show?
    *Druhá strana:* The same stillness can turn into a hand that never lets the pen come down.
