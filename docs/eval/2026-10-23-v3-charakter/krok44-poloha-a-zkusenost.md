## 2. Materiál

Runy Fehu (obojí), Isa (příroda), Othila (situace); u každé 3 zkušenosti z GPT knihovny (těžká · dobrá · neutrální), ke každé obraz z banky
v2 (`RUNE_IMAGES`). Každá zkušenost ve všech třech polohách = 9 čtení na polohu. Pokyn (Isa · ticho ve vztahu · poloha A):

```
RUNE: ISA (ice)
WHAT THIS READING SHOWS OF ISA (one human experience that lies in its meaning; say it in your own words, do not copy it): a relationship in which no one says anything any more
THE IMAGE (a scene that carries that experience; tell it in your own words): The clock on the wall has stopped and no one has wound it.
Say nothing as fact about the reader's own life: no named family roles (father, mother, grandmother and so on), no past or feeling of the reader stated as fact. "Someone close", "a friend" or "family" as a whole are fine.

THE READING: 4 sentences, 50 to 60 words, in this order:
1: what ISA means here, in plain words.
2: the image. Begin it with "Imagine" and place the reader inside the scene, as the one standing in it.
3: the shadow: a danger that lies inside this same meaning, not a fault of the reader.
4: one open question that different readers could take in different ways.
Reply with the reading only.
```
Poloha B: `Begin it with "Imagine" and let the reader watch someone else in the scene.` · poloha C: `Show the scene as it is, without "Imagine"; if a person is in it, it is someone, not the reader.`
