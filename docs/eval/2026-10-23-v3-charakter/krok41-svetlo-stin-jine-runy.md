2. **Fehu · Love & Relationships · světlý úhel · svět oblasti · Decision ahead · Clarity · humor** (57) Wealth can be work, care or a skill that grows as it passes on, like a ladder lent next door that comes back with a jar of jam. Nobody asked for the jam. Lend it to the wrong door, and it stays in their garage for good. When the next knock comes, what will you carry over?
   SHADOW: Giving only to get something back is just trade with a smile.
3. **Fehu · Crossroads & Decisions · tmavý úhel · vlastní obraz nabídnut · Understanding the past · Insight into Challenge** (59) Wealth can drain as easily as it fills, and held too tightly it turns to worry and quarrel, like a full pail two people grab by the handle. The harder they pull, the more spills. The light is this: the water stays only if one lets go, which feels like losing. When something filled before, whose hands tightened first?
4. **Kenaz · Inner Growth · světlý úhel · svět oblasti · Right now · Reflection** (57) A small light shows what was there all along: hold a torch close to an old wall, and a crack that took years to grow is suddenly plain. Nothing new appeared; only the seeing changed. Now you cannot walk past that wall without knowing. What has the light reached in you that you used to walk past?
   SHADOW: Some things were easier to live with in the dark.
5. **Kenaz · Career & Creativity · světlý úhel · svět oblasti · Decision ahead · Confirmation** (56) Kenaz here is understanding that turns into making: a joiner tilts a board toward the window, sees how the grain runs, and picks up the pencil.    SHADOW: Understanding can become a reason never to begin.
6. **Kenaz · Healing & Wellbeing · tmavý úhel · vlastní obraz nabídnut · Understanding the past · General Guidance** (58) Kenaz can be light that shows what you would rather not see: a candle held close to the sock you are darning. The flame does not make the holes; it only refuses to hide them. Yet a hole you can see is one you can mend. What did you do when a small light found a worn place?
7. **Hagalaz · Crossroads & Decisions · tmavý úhel · svět oblasti · Right now · Clarity** (55) Hagalaz here is a disruption nobody invited and nobody can steer, like the call that comes the night before you sign the new lease. Look at the pen still lying on the table, uncapped. Nothing is signed yet, so the hail lands before the ink. What does that pen feel like in your hand now?
8. **Hagalaz · Family & Home · tmavý úhel · svět oblasti · Decision ahead · Insight into Challenge** (57) What is left standing after hail shows which roots were truly firm, like the summer after Grandma's funeral, when only two cousins still come to the lake house. The others came for her, not for each other. The empty chairs hurt; the filled ones are real. Do you set the table for two, or call the others?
9. **Hagalaz · Healing & Wellbeing · tmavý úhel · vlastní obraz nabídnut · Understanding the past · Reflection** (59) After a storm the land is not the same, some things gone and others laid bare, like a field the morning after hail. 10. **Laguz · The Unseen · světlý úhel · svět oblasti · Right now · Confirmation · humor** (58) Laguz here is the part of you that knows before you can explain it, like a cook lifting the milk off the stove just before it boils over, her back turned.    SHADOW: Not every hunch is knowing; some are only fear in a hurry.
11. **Laguz · Love & Relationships · světlý úhel · svět oblasti · Decision ahead · General Guidance** (58) Water takes the shape of whatever holds it and is still water, like a woman who walks slowly beside her old father and quickly beside her son. 12. **Laguz · Crossroads & Decisions · tmavý úhel · vlastní obraz nabídnut · Understanding the past · Clarity** (51) Not every feeling is true: like a stream splitting around a stone, it can run deep and murky in both channels. The depth is real; what lies beneath it is not shown. Yet water that keeps moving can settle clear. What did the last dark current you followed carry you to?

```
RUNE: FEHU (wealth, cattle, material prosperity)
WHAT THIS READING SHOWS OF FEHU (one of its meanings; say it in your own words): wealth that is alive: it needs care every day, and having it is not the same as drawing from it. This is a light side of FEHU.
Say nothing as fact about the reader's own life.

THE IMAGE: one scene from family ties and belonging that a person could stop and look at, where something you can see carries this meaning — a moment between people, a situation, a thing at work, whatever fits this meaning best. Say it the way a person would say it aloud: plain, natural words.

THE READING — 4 sentences, 50 to 58 words, in this order:
- 1: that meaning of FEHU, said so clearly that it is understood even without the image, and in the same sentence the image.
- 2: what the image shows of that meaning, briefly; do not describe the scene further.
- 3: what it costs, or where it turns dark, still in that image.
- 4: one open question, about what is there now, that grows out of the image and that different readers could take in different ways.
After the reading, on a new line beginning SHADOW:, one short sentence: the shadow side of this meaning, said as a plain thought, not a scene. Write it only if it adds something sentence 3 has not said; otherwise leave the line out.
Reply with the reading (50 to 58 words) and, if it adds something, that one line; nothing else.
```
