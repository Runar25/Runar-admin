## 3. Co z toho plyne

1. Záměr patří do otázky, ne do obrazu — vložky času zmizely bez ztráty.
2. Los druhu obrazu a svět oblasti se perou: když druh určí obraz, oblast se ztratí (5/12). Návrh: druh obrazu určí **jak** obraz
   funguje, oblast **odkud** je — obojí v jedné větě pokynu („a still state, from the world of family and home“).
3. Isa potřebuje k úhlu výslovně říct, že obraz může být i jiný než zima — jinak vlastní obraz runy vyhraje i tam, kde nebyl nabídnut.
   (Pozor na paměť „nepojmenuj v promptu, co hned zakážeš“ — řešit kladně: nabídnout prostředí, ne zakázat led.)

## 5. Čtení

1. **Fehu · Career · děj · teď · General** (56) Fehu is wealth that lives only while it moves, like millrace water that turns the wheel only as it runs past. Hold it back to keep it, and the wheel stands still. Yet open the sluice too wide and the pond drains, leaving nothing to push. What is the water doing at your wheel right now?
2. **Fehu · Family · situace · před rozhodnutím · Clarity · humor** (57) Fehu is value that can be work, attention or a bond, like a man spending his whole Saturday gluing a chair nobody sits in. The worth sits in the hours, not the chair. But hours given where no one looks start to feel like a debt. Does he fix the next one, or sit in this one?
3. **Fehu · The Unseen · stav · minulost · Insight** (56) Fehu is not only what you hold but what you do with it: a woodshed stacked to the roof beside a cold stove. Stacked, the wood is only the shape of warmth; it becomes wealth in the burning. Burning empties the shed, and wood saved too long goes grey with damp. What have you left unburned?
4. **Uruz · Healing · protiklad · teď · Reflection** (57) Uruz is life in the body that keeps going when the way is hard, like grass pushing through cracked asphalt, not a cut flower in a vase. The stone gives no room; the blade grows anyway, slowly. It comes up bent and thin, and it cannot choose another place. Where in you is something pushing up now?
5. **Uruz · Crossroads · zákonitost · před rozhodnutím · Confirmation** (57) Uruz is a time that forces change, like a hermit crab whose shell grows tight before it has found another. Waiting does not loosen the old shell, and the next one lies somewhere out on open sand. Between shells the crab is soft, and the gulls can see it. How will you know which empty shell fits?
6. **Uruz · Inner Growth · tělo · minulost · General** (58) Uruz is strength that comes only when needed, like the arm that catches a falling child before you think. You did not know it was there; the need called it, and it came. Afterward the arm shakes, and you cannot summon that grip again to prove it. What have your hands done that you never knew they could?
7. **Gebo · Love · vlastní obraz · teď · Clarity** (57) A gift binds two people the way one hand giving and another taking makes a single motion between them. Watch the moment the hand closes on the gift, because taking it is already a promise back. If only one hand ever opens, the giver grows tired and the taker grows heavy. Which hand is open right now?
8. **Gebo · Family · děj · před rozhodnutím · Insight · humor** (57) A gift is worth what you let go of, like a bowl of shelled walnuts worth exactly one sore thumbnail. The longer you sit cracking, the more of your evening ends up in the bowl. Those who eat them never see the shells, and that stings. Will you crack the next bowl, or set out the nutcracker?
9. **Gebo · Crossroads · situace · minulost · Reflection** (58) A gift counts only if it could have been kept, like a woman giving up her bus seat because the driver told her to. She stands all the way home, and the seat feels taken, not given. Choosing freely would not have made her legs ache less. When did you last give way, and whose choice was it?
10. **Isa · Healing · stav · teď · Confirmation** (57) Isa is the stop where nothing can be hurried, like a frozen pond in January, every leaf held still under the ice. The water has not gone; it waits, and you see straight to the bottom. But ice that lasts too long keeps the fish from air. What do you see down there, now that nothing moves?
11. **Isa · Inner Growth · protiklad · před rozhodnutím · General** (58) Some things cannot be hurried: a frozen river is not a dry bed, for under the still ice the water keeps moving. Stamping on the ice will not bring the thaw sooner; the current works unseen. But left too long, the ice thickens and less water gets through. When the ice cracks, do you step out or wait?
12. **Isa · Love · zákonitost · minulost · Clarity** (58) The urge to force things to move works like a stuck zipper: the harder you yank, the tighter the teeth bite. What comes clear is the hand, not the zipper, pulling quicker at each catch. A hard tug sometimes frees it, but often the cloth tears and nothing closes. Last time something caught, what did your hand do?
