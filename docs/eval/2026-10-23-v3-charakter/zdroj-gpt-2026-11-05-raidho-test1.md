# Zdroj: 10 čtení Raidho z GPT, test 1: tenké zadání + definice obrazu (owner 2026-11-05)

Prompt = tenké zadání (`prompt-v3-navrh.md` oddíl J) + definice obrazu z oddílu G se světem „everyday situation“. Intake, ne kánon. Text doslova. Rozbor → tamtéž, oddíl M.

1. Imagine following a recipe while cooking dinner. Each ingredient goes in at the right stage, and the food slowly comes together. Add everything at once and the order is lost. Raidho represents movement with direction and timing. A process works when each step arrives at the moment it belongs.

2. Imagine driving through a busy intersection. Cars move in different directions, but traffic flows because each driver waits for the right moment. One car ignores the lights, and everything stops. Raidho represents movement guided by direction and rhythm. Without that order, movement creates obstacles instead of progress.

3. Imagine assembling a piece of furniture. You spread the parts across the floor and follow the instructions, one stage at a time. The frame begins to take shape. Rush ahead and some pieces will not fit. Raidho represents a process whose direction depends on the order of its steps.

4. Imagine a train arriving at a station. Passengers get off before others board, and the doors close when the exchange is finished. The train moves on. Raidho represents movement through timing and sequence. When one stage is delayed, everything behind it must wait, even when the destination remains clear.

5. Imagine learning to ride a bicycle. At first, you push too hard, turn too sharply, and lose your balance. Then your movements begin to work together. The bicycle moves forward without constant correction. Raidho represents direction and rhythm. Movement becomes progress when each action finds its proper moment.

6. Imagine watering a garden with a hose. You move from one dry patch to another, covering the ground in an orderly way. But a leaking connection sends water behind you, leaving the plants ahead dry. Raidho represents movement with direction. Motion alone does not mean that anything is being reached.

7. Imagine a queue at a supermarket checkout. One person finishes paying, gathers their bags, and leaves room for the next customer. The line moves steadily. Then someone cannot find their wallet, and everyone waits. Raidho represents movement through sequence and timing. One interruption can change the rhythm of everything behind it.

8. Imagine cycling to work through streets you know well. You catch the green lights and keep a steady pace. Then you take a wrong turn and spend the next few streets correcting it. Raidho represents movement with direction. A great deal of effort can be spent recovering from a lost course.

9. Imagine baking bread. You mix the ingredients, knead the dough, and leave it to rise before putting it in the oven. Taking it out too early leaves it heavy and dense. Raidho represents the rhythm of a process and the right moment. Some things cannot be hurried into readiness.

10. Imagine moving boxes into a new flat. You carry the heavy furniture first, then bring in the smaller things that belong around it. The rooms begin to take shape. If you start with the wrong things, you keep moving boxes twice. Raidho represents direction, sequence, and knowing what needs to happen first.
