Plyne: (1) vada „promise“ byla ze slova v pokynu — po přepisu zmizela; (2) výběr konce „podle čtení“ nefunguje: z pěti druhů model
skoro vždy vezme jeden (detail) — podle pravidla stanoveného předem → druh konce se musí losovat; (3) konec (c) „co runa žádá“ nezazněl
ani jednou, takže jeho riziko (rada) zatím neznáme — změří se, až ho los vynutí.

- Gebo gift: A stranger leans into your bumper in the snow, the wheels catch, and he walks off with one raised hand. Here Gebo is help handed over with no bill attached. What the picture leaves out is the weight of a debt with no one to pay it to. His footprints in the drift, already filling with snow.
- Gebo balance: On a cold evening two people sit under one blanket, and each lets go of a little edge so the wool reaches the other. Gebo is a gift that stays even only while each side gives up a little. Neither is fully warm; a shoulder or a foot stays out in the cold. The blanket just reaches both.
- Gebo giving and receiving: You lend the neighbour your trailer; in autumn a leg of lamb turns up on your step. Gebo keeps things passing between two houses, each return in a different shape. Nobody weighed trailer against lamb, and the balance holds only while neither side keeps count. The trailer is back in your yard, waiting.
- Isa waiting: The pond has frozen over, and under the ice the water stays where it is, asking nothing. Isa holds a thing in place until the cold lets go. What is held cannot move, and nothing reaches it, not a boat, not a duck coming down to drink. Is the pond resting, or is it shut?
- Isa clarity through cold: In the hard frost the air holds nothing, and the farm across the fjord stands so near you could count its fence posts. Cold strips the air of haze, so what was far away shows sharp. That sharpness comes with no warmth, and the water between is still just as wide. Every fence post is there, out of reach.
- Isa pause: The boat sits locked in harbour ice, and past the harbour mouth the fjord moves dark and open. Isa stops what would move, and keeps it whole until the ice lets go. The open water lies in plain sight, and the hull cannot reach it. The mooring rope hangs slack, frozen into the same ice.
- Tiwaz justice: At the kiosk the change comes back with too much in it, and you slide the extra coin back across the counter. Tiwaz here is setting the scale level again, even when the tilt was yours. Nobody would have known, and you walk home lighter than you might have. The coin lies on the counter, between two hands.
- Tiwaz sacrifice: At the fork you went left, and when you look back the right track has gone into the fog. Tiwaz gives up one road so the other can be walked whole. The cost is the right track itself: what lay along it stays unseen, and the fog does not give it back. Under your feet, only the left track.
- Tiwaz courage: Wind at the door, the flock is counted, one is missing, and you pull the coat back on. Tiwaz is the step taken toward danger because something out there is yours to answer for. The warm room stays behind you, and the storm does not care why you came. The gate swings shut on the rest of the flock.
