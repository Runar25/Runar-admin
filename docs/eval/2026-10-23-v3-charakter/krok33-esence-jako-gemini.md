a obě odejdou bohatší, žádná nepohltí druhou (Gemini) · rovnováha dávání a braní, ne obchod a účetnictví (ownerův popis Gebo
v `RUNAR_POPISY_RUN.md`) · dát znamená pustit něco, co bylo tvoje · vynucený dar není dar, dávat z plného poháru (owner, Gemini) ·
druhá strana: dar zavazuje, lze dávat, aby svázal, a brát tak, že tiše váže.

## 3. Krok 33 — příklad bez určení druhu (6 čtení)

1. Věta 1 = princip + všední příklad v téže větě · věta 2 = A: princip v oblasti (práce · vztahy · vnitřní stav), B: princip o krok dál ·
   věta 3 = stín · věta 4 = otevřená otázka.
2. **Výsledek:** soused ve všech 6 čteních (5× půjčený žebřík, 1× gauč) — stejná vada, jakou owner vytkl krokům 32.
   Otázka „What leaves your hand when you give?“ 3× ze 6 — ozvěna podkladu („something leaves the hand“).

Čtení:
1. **A práce** (56 slov) A gift opens a bond between two people, the way a lent ladder means one day a knock on your door. In work, the one who teaches an apprentice gives hours and gets back hands that carry the trade further. A gift given to hold someone becomes a leash. What leaves your hand when you give?
2. **A vztahy** (58) A gift asks for a gift: the neighbor who lends his ladder knocks when his own roof leaks. Between two people it is an open bridge, where each stays whole and both cross back richer. But a gift can hide a hook; kindness given to hold someone binds tighter than rope. Which gift is still in your hands?
3. **A nitro** (58) A gift asks for a gift, like the neighbor who lends his ladder and one day knocks for your hands. You can only give from a full cup; from an empty one, nothing leaves. But a gift can be a rope, given to hold or taken so that it quietly ties. What leaves your hand when you give?
4. **B 1** (58) A gift asks for a gift: when a neighbor lends you his ladder, something now runs between your two houses. When it holds, each stays their own person and each walks away richer, with no ledger kept. But every gift carries a hook, and one given to bind holds like rope. What leaves your hand when you give?
5. **B 2** (58) A gift asks for a gift, like a neighbor who lends his ladder and later knocks when he needs a hand. When it holds, both stay themselves and both walk away richer. But a gift can be a hook, given to hold someone or taken so it binds without a word. What did your last gift cost you?
6. **B 3** (59) A gift calls for a gift: the neighbor who helps you carry a sofa will one day knock with boxes of his own. Between the two a thread is tied, and each stays whole while holding more. But a gift can be a hook, given so the other cannot later say no. Who holds the end of the thread?

## 4. Krok 34 — příklad = obraz z hmotného světa (6 čtení)

Změna jen ve větě 1 a 4 (celý pokyn, varianta stavba):

```
- 1: what Gebo is — its principle, said plainly, carried in the same sentence by one image from the material world
     (how something is built and stands) that works the same way. The image is a thing or a process, not an anecdote.
- 2: what happens between the two sides when it holds — the principle carried one step further.
- 3: its other side — where the gift stops being free, said of how it works, not of the reader.
- 4: one open question that grows out of the image and that different readers could take in different ways.
```

Los druhu obrazu: stavba · příroda · běžná věc · tělo · 2× bez losu. Podklad o runě bez věty „dar není jídlo“ (u obrazů z hmotného světa
není potřeba) a bez obratu „leaves the hand“.

1. **Výsledek:**
   1. Lidé ani domácí scénka v žádném ze 6 čtení. Obrazy: krokve, řeka a pole, most 2×, dech, lano.
   2. Otázka roste z obrazu v 6/6 („What does the field owe the river?“, „Which rafter is leaning, and which is holding?“).
   3. **Nová vada — věty 2 a 3 opisují podklad:** „neither swallows the other“ doslova ve 4 ze 6, „taken … quietly binds/ties“ ve 3 ze 6;
      věta 3 skoro vždy hák / lano / vodítko. Začátek „Gebo is a gift that asks for a gift“ / „Gebo is giving that calls back a gift“ / „A gift calls for a gift“ ve 4 ze 6.
   4. Běžná věc dala most (ne předmět z domácnosti) — los domény model nevzal doslova.
   5. Délka 56–59 slov (1× přes 58).
2. **Hranice:** n = 6, jedno čtení na variantu; nic z toho není změřený rozdíl, jen co je na vzorku vidět. Soudí owner.

Čtení:
1. **stavba** (56) Gebo is a gift that asks for a gift, like two rafters meeting at the ridge, each standing because the other leans back. When it holds, both stand richer, and neither swallows the other. But a gift can be given to bind, or taken so it quietly binds. Which rafter is leaning, and which is holding?
2. **příroda** (59) A gift opens a tie between two, the way a river feeds a field and the field returns its water as rain. When it holds, both sides come away fuller and neither swallows the other. But a gift can be given as a hook, and taken in a way that quietly binds. What does the field owe the river?
3. **běžná věc** (57) Gebo is giving that calls back a gift, as a bridge stands only when both banks bear its weight. Each side lets go of something it owned, and both grow richer while staying two shores. But a bridge can be built to bind, and the crossing quietly becomes a debt. Who decides when the toll is paid?
4. **tělo** (58) Gebo is a gift that asks for a gift, as lungs must let their breath go before they can draw the next. Between two who give this way a tie forms, and each comes away fuller, neither swallowed. But a gift can be held out like a hook, binding whoever takes it. What breath are you still holding?
5. **bez losu 1** (57) A gift calls for a gift, the way a bridge needs ground on both banks. When it holds, each side lets go of something, and both come away fuller, neither swallowed. But a gift can also be a rope, given to bind and taken in a way that quietly ties. What crosses your bridge, and which way?
6. **bez losu 2** (56) Gebo is the gift that ties two together, like a rope thrown across a gap that holds only when the far side takes hold. When it holds, both pull, and neither is dragged onto the other's bank. But a rope thrown to haul someone in is a leash. Which end of the rope do you hold?
