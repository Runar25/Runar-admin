## C
```
RUNE: GEBO

SEMANTIC FOUNDATION
- Gift and exchange.
- Giving and receiving between parties.
- Reciprocity does not necessarily imply equal value.
- A gift may create expectations, but this is not inevitable.

INTERPRETIVE POSSIBILITIES
- An offer that has not yet been accepted.
- An exchange whose value cannot be measured equally.
- A gift whose meaning changes after it is received.
- The difference between giving freely and giving with an expectation.

BOUNDARIES
- Do not assume the reading concerns romantic relationships.
- Do not automatically treat obligation as negative.
- Do not reduce the rune to harmony, partnership, or generosity.
- Do not present these possibilities as historical facts.

TASK
Create a reading using the supplied material as a source of possibilities, not as a checklist.
Choose a coherent direction and develop it through a concrete image. Do not attempt to include every possible meaning.
```

## D = C + před TASK
```
IMAGE UNITS (starting points, not sentences to copy)
- handing over: a hand passing something across a table — what crosses from one side to the other
- receiving: an open palm that stays empty — a gift can be taken, or refused
- obligation: an object kept although it is no longer wanted — when a gift becomes a duty
- imbalance: one side keeps bringing more — must an exchange be equal
- delayed return: an empty place on the table where something is meant to come back — an expectation never spoken
- irreversibility: an object that cannot return to how it was once given — what the handing over itself changes
```

Plyne (n = 3 na variantu, jen Gebo, jen Opus):
1. Bez materiálu (A, B) model o Gebo vykládá — tvar runy, „starý zvyk“, žádný obraz, a radí. S materiálem GPT (C, D) postaví krátkou
   scénu a z ní je jasné, co Gebo představuje (2/2 u všech šesti). To je přesně ownerovo „krátký obraz, který vysvětlí, co v něm Gebo je“.
2. Ale všechna tři čtení uvnitř C mají tutéž scénu i tutéž myšlenku (první možnost ze seznamu: nabídka ještě nepřijatá) a všechna tři v D

3. Knihovna obrazů (D) obraz zkrátila, ale model ji použil jako hotový obraz (hrnek přes stůl 3/3) — jednotka se chová jako věta k opsání.

## Texty

**A — jen jméno**
- A1: Gebo is two staves crossed, each holding the other up. It is the rune of the gift, and a gift in the old way asks for one back. Something is given, something is returned, and the bond lives in the passing between hands. Gebo has no reversal; turn it any way and it stands the same.
- A2: Gebo is two strokes crossing, and neither one holds the other up alone. It is the gift, and the gift that comes back. Among the old people a gift called for a return; the bond was made by giving and kept by giving back. This rune has no reversal; turn it any way and it reads the same.
- A3: Gebo is two strokes crossed, neither one lying under the other. It is the gift, and in the old way a gift was never one hand only. What is given binds the giver and the one who takes, the way a shared meal binds a table. Look at what is passing between hands, and which way it travels.

**B — jméno + definice**
- B1: Gebo is two strokes crossing, each holding the other up. A gift sits in the rune, and in the old way a gift calls for a gift. What is given and what is received weigh the same here. Look at what passes between your hands and another's, and let it keep passing.
- B2: Gebo is two strokes crossing, each holding the other up. A gift passes from one hand into another, and something comes back along the same road. Taking is half of it; the rune does not stand on one leg. Look at what is in your hands now, and at who placed it there.
- B3: Gebo is two strokes crossing, and neither one stands without the other. A gift passes from one hand to another, and something goes back the other way. Bread for labor, a seat at the fire for a story told. The rune asks only this: what comes in, and what goes out?

**C — materiál GPT**
- C1: A bowl of fish sits on the doorstep, and the door has not yet opened. Until a hand takes it, it is only fish going cold. Once it is carried inside, it becomes something owed, or something shared, or simply supper. The giver set it down; what it turns into belongs to the one who lifts it.
- C2: A bowl of bread sits on the threshold, set down by one hand, not yet lifted by another. Gebo is that space between the setting down and the taking up. Once the bowl is carried inside, it is no longer only bread. What it becomes depends on who lifts it, and why.
- C3: A loaf left on a neighbour's step is not yet a gift; it is only bread waiting for a hand. Gebo stands at that step, where the offer is made and the taking has not happened. Once the door opens, the bread carries a question back across the threshold. What returns need not weigh the same as what was given.

**D — materiál GPT + knihovna obrazů**
- D1: A cup is passed across the table, and on the other side it is a different cup. It left one hand as an object and arrives as a gift. What it means now belongs partly to the hand that holds it. Nothing on the table asks for a return, and Gebo does not say whether one will come.
- D2: A hand passes a cup across the table, and the cup is no longer the same cup. On this side it was something owned; on that side it is something given. Nothing has to come back for the exchange to be real. What the crossing changes stays changed, whether or not the other hand asked for it.
- D3: A cup crosses the table from one hand to another. Once it has crossed, it is no longer the cup that stood on the giver's side. Whether a thank-you, a debt, or nothing comes back, the passing has already changed it. Gebo marks the moment of handing over, not what it is worth.
