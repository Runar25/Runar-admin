## F0 (krok 6)
```
- A gift (the rune's name is the old word for "gift").
- In the old poems a gift looks for a gift in return.
- Giving and giving back is what makes and keeps a bond between people.
```
## F1 — prosté, dnešní, bez „old poems“ a etymologie
```
- Gebo is the rune of the gift: something passes from one person to another, and a bond begins with it.
- A gift often calls for a gift back, and giving back keeps the bond alive.
```
## F2 — nejkratší
```
- Gebo: a gift, and the bond it makes between the one who gives and the one who receives.
```
