# V3 lab, krok 26 — situační obraz bez „…that costs the one who does it something“ (4 čtení Gebo, Opus 5.5)

2026-10-27, CODE-read. Owner: „nelíbí se mi, že tam model cpe, že se to stalo v sobotu“ · „asi zkus test a uvidíme“. Jediná změna proti
kroku 25: z řádku situačního obrazu vypadla cena (patří do věty hranice). Fyzický obraz se nemění, proto běží jen 4 situační čtení se
stejnými losy jako kroky 24–25.

| # | druh činu | krok 25 (s cenou v obrazu) | krok 26 (bez ceny) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | půjčit, co sám potřebuješ | Your neighbour asks for the ladder on the Saturday you meant to clear your gutters, and you carry it over. Gebo lives in that walk, a pull between keeping and handing over. It holds until the ladder stays away a week and the giving starts keeping count. When you hand something over, what do you usually wait for? | You lend your neighbour your ladder on Friday, though your own gutters still need clearing before the rain. While it leans on his wall, giving and keeping pull at each other, binding you both. It stops holding when only one side ever lends, and the gutters overflow. When something of yours stays away, what do you usually do? |
| 3 | naučit, co umíš | Gebo is the bond that forms when something passes from one person to another. You kneel in the hallway showing a child how to tie a lace, again and again, while your coffee goes cold. Once she ties it alone, she runs off, and nothing comes back to you. What might someone still learn from your hands? | Gebo is the bond made when something passes between two people and leaves both changed. You hold the back of a bike seat while a child pedals, then let go without telling her. Once she rides alone, she no longer needs your hand on the seat. What are you still holding that might be ready to go? |
| 5 | čekat na opozdilce | You stand at the station, the train long gone, still waiting for the friend who said ten minutes. The waiting becomes your time handed over, a gift with no wrapping. It holds until the cold reaches your feet and you start counting what you are owed. How long can you stand there before it stops being given? | You stand by the station doors, checking the clock, still there twenty minutes past the hour. Your staying becomes the gift: time handed over that you will not get back. It holds while the other knows you stayed; unseen, it sours into a grudge. How long would you stand there before the waiting asks to be paid? |
| 7 | nést náklad s někým | Gebo is a gift that binds: what passes between two hands leaves a thread. A neighbour sees you hauling a sofa upstairs, takes the other end, and walks backwards on a sore knee. When only one side ever lifts, the thread becomes a weight one carries alone. Who took the other end for you, and what still ties you? | Gebo is giving that binds: whoever takes is tied to the one who gave. Two neighbours tip a sofa up the stairwell, one walking backwards, calling out each step. If one of them only shouts directions while the other bears the weight, the tie frays into resentment. Who is at the other end of what you carry? |

**Výsledek (n = 4):** čas jako cena v obraze 3/4 → 2/4 — „Saturday“ zmizela, ale objevil se „Friday“ (1) a „twenty minutes past the hour“ (5).
Zbytek pochází z **losovaného druhu činu**: „půjčit, co sám potřebuješ“ a „čekat na opozdilce“ v sobě čas nesou. Odebrání ceny pomohlo jen
napůl; zbytek je v textu losu. Hranice znovu 2× „It holds / stops holding“ — ozvěna zadání.
