## 5. Druhy obrazu, které vysvětlují význam (odpověď na ownerovu otázku)

Ve v2 jsou ratifikované tři typy (`RUNAR_DESIGN.md` „Typ obrazu“): **fyzický** (věc koná — děj), **situační** (člověk koná a čin něco
stojí), **slovní** (výpověď o stavu, jen pilot). Owner správně vidí, že v2 převládá děj. Možnosti pro v3, s ukázkami z kroků 35–37:

1. **Děj** (v2 výchozí) — něco se děje v hmotě: „a river in spring breaking the ice it slept under“.
2. **Situace** (v2 situační) — člověk v jedné chvíli, kdy ho to něco stojí: „a voice staying level while the one you love says
   something hard“.
3. **Stav** — nic se neděje, je vidět, jak věc *je*: „coals banked for the night, keeping their heat under grey ash“, „a sketch pinned
   above the desk that the hand keeps passing by“.
4. **Protiklad** — co runa je proti tomu, čím není: „not a fire gone out but coals banked for the night“, „not the polished strength of
   a finished blade but the raw iron glowing on the anvil“.
5. **Zákonitost** — jak věci obecně chodí (system v3 ji dovoluje: „You may say how a thing generally works“): „a saw that cuts while
   used and rusts on the hook“, „a bridge stands only when both banks bear its weight“.
6. **Tělesný pocit** — co dělá tělo: „the shaking comes after“, „lungs must let their breath go before they can draw the next“.
7. **Vlastní obraz runy** — z ownerova popisu (voda do nádoby, led, pratur, dvě ruce); jedna z možností, ne povinnost.

Návrh: losovat **druh** (1–6) a vedle něj dál nabízet vlastní obraz runy; každý druh je jedna řádka pokynu (data, §18).

## 6. Čtení

1. **Fehu · Love · vlastní obraz · teď · General** (57) Fehu is worth that lives only while it moves, like water filling a jug and now spilling over the rim. In love, what passes back and forth stays warm; what is kept back for safety goes stale like standing water. Yet a jug that only pours runs dry. Where does the overflow run, and who drinks it?
2. **Fehu · Healing · obraz z oblasti · před rozhodnutím · Clarity** (57) What is fed grows and what is left standing weakens, like a stiff knee after weeks on the sofa, about to meet the stairs. Strength goes where attention and use go, and it leaves quietly from the rest. But worry, fed every night, grows as well. What in you is waiting at the foot of those stairs?
3. **Fehu · Crossroads · vlastní obraz · minulost · Insight** (53) Fehu shows something starting to open, like water that has filled the jug and begun to spill over its rim. What has begun to grow keeps growing only where nothing presses it back. At a crossroads, making room for one thing means turning away from another. Which way will the spilling water run?
4. **Uruz · Love · obraz z oblasti · teď · Reflection** (57) Uruz is strength that comes when it is needed, not strength for show, like a voice staying level while the one you love says something hard. Nobody sees it built; it is simply there when you reach for it. It rarely outlasts the moment; the shaking comes after. When your voice held, who was it holding for?
5. **Uruz · Career · vlastní obraz · před rozhodnutím · Confirmation** (57) The wild aurochs has outgrown its pen, its shoulders already pressing the rails, as the old way of working stops fitting. What comes next has no shape yet, only open ground under heavy hooves. Strength like that breaks the fence but does not draw the map. Which way does the beast turn once the rails are down?
6. **Uruz · Family · obraz z oblasti · minulost · General** (57) Uruz is a body's strength that keeps going when the way is hard, like a father who carried the sleeping child upstairs after a double shift. Such strength does not wait to feel ready; it simply moves. But a body never allowed to stop will one day stop on its own. Who carries whom up the stairs?
7. **Gebo · Career · vlastní obraz · teď · Clarity** (58) A gift weighs something because part of you goes with it: one hand opens, another closes around the hours you put in. In work that is made, what you hand over is rarely the thing itself but the attention it took. Give only what costs nothing and the other hand stays empty. Which hand is yours right now?
8. **Gebo · Inner Growth · obraz z oblasti · před rozhodnutím · Insight** (54) Gebo can mean accepting a thing before knowing its use, like a student pocketing a teacher's words just before the door closes. The taking comes first; the understanding may arrive years later, or never. Carry too much unopened and it becomes dead weight that teaches nothing. What is he carrying out of that room?
9. **Gebo · The Unseen · vlastní obraz · minulost · Reflection** (56) One hand has already given and another has taken, and what passed between them now belongs to neither. Neither can carry it home alone; it lives only while both hands stay open. Close a fist to keep it, and you hold only a palm already cooling. When you reach out, which of your hands moves first?
10. **Isa · Career · obraz z oblasti · teď · Confirmation** (56) Isa is not an end but a time not yet come, like a sketch pinned above the desk that the hand keeps passing by. What lies frozen is kept whole, holding its shape until the thaw. But waited on too long, ice keeps nothing except the cold. What would make the hand stop at that sketch?
11. **Isa · Love · vlastní obraz · před rozhodnutím · General** (57) Isa can be the urge to force things to move, like a river pushing up under its ice in March, the surface about to crack. Between two people, it is wanting an answer, a decision, a step forward, now. Ice forced open breaks jagged, and someone gets wet. Who is standing on the ice when it goes?
12. **Isa · Purpose · obraz z oblasti · minulost · Clarity** (58) When everything stops, one thing is left in front of you, like the day the office closed and only the sketchbook stayed on the cleared desk. Ice gives no road; it holds one thing still enough to see. Stay frozen too long and the thing you stare at hardens into a wall. What is that sketchbook waiting for?

Pokyn (Isa · Career; ostatní se liší runou, úhlem, oblastí a losy):

```
RUNE: ISA
WHAT THIS READING SHOWS OF ISA (one of its meanings; say it in your own words): not an end, but a moment before its time has come.
THE READING IS FOR: Career & Creativity — let the meaning land there; say nothing as fact about the reader's own life.

THE READING — 4 sentences, 50 to 58 words, in this order:
- 1: that meaning of ISA, said plainly, and in the same sentence one image from the part of life the reading is for that carries it;
     the image shows something happening now.
- 2: the meaning said clearly, one step further than sentence 1. Here: neither confirm nor refute anything.
- 3: where it stops holding, or what it costs.
- 4: one open question that grows out of the image and that different readers could take in different ways.
```
