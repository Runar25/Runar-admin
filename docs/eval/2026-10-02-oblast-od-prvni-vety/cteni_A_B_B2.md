## Fehu × Love & Relationships

### A — dnešní prompt: „nech obraz přistát na oblasti“

**sol 6 (tvoje čtení v appce)**  
> At the farm gate, the cow changes hands before the first frost. What she fetches keeps bread on your table through winter, Kuky, though her stall stands empty. Fehu holds value that can serve you when it passes from one hand to another. In love, what you give and receive may be keeping you both fed, or leaving one place bare.  
> ✦ Where does what you value begin to grow when you share it?

**Opus 5 (testeři)**  
> At the autumn market the cow changes hands, and what she was worth becomes bread and firewood through the dark months. Fehu is wealth that only counts once it moves. What stays in the byre, Kuky, feeds no one but itself. Between you and another, what you hold back may be worth less than what you hand over.  
> ✦ Fehu pours slowly, then suddenly the cup is full — where in your giving are you still measuring the first drops?

**sol 6.1**  
> At your byre’s doorway, the cow passes into another hand, her price becoming winter grain. On the empty stall’s latch, you can still see a pale tuft of hair. Fehu names value in circulation, Kuky, rather than possession alone. What you give in love may return as something different, yet still offer you sustenance.  
> ✦ How does the value of what you give change when it reaches another’s hands?

## Kenaz × Family & Home

### A — dnešní prompt: „nech obraz přistát na oblasti“

**sol 6 (tvoje čtení v appce)**  
> You see the whole workbench beneath a single lamp, then only the worn place where a hand rests. Kenaz is the fire that makes one small thing clear. At home, you may notice whose hand leaves that mark, and whose does not. Among your people, this may be a closeness with one place still unshared.  
> ✦ What comes into view for you when the light reaches just one step farther?

**Opus 5 (testeři)**  
> The room holds tools, shelves, a cold stove, a door — then all of it goes dark but the one bright circle on the workbench. Kenaz is the light small enough to show only what is under your hands. In a household, what the lamp falls on gets tended, and the rest waits in the dark corners. It may be that in your family one thing is clearly seen now, and that seeing cannot be undone.  
> ✦ What stands a step ahead of you now, lit just enough to make out — and how long was that door there before you saw it?

**sol 6.1**  
> You see the bench beneath a single lamp, then only the worn place beside your own. Kenaz names the clarity that makes one small thing worth a closer look. In that light, you can distinguish wear from damage without pretending they are the same. Among your people, disagreement may not mean a damaged bond.  
> ✦ What possibility can you see now, even without seeing what lies beyond it?

### B — jen jiná věta o oblasti: „scénu postav v oblasti od první věty, z obrazu jen děj“

Kenaz speaks of seeing one thing clearly without claiming to see everything. Among your people, this may be a time when one small detail is harder to overlook.  
> ✦ What comes into view when you look just one step ahead?

**Opus 5 (testeři)**

Everything else falls away but what lies under that light, the one piece in your hands. Kenaz is the small fire that shows a thing clearly rather than warming the whole room. Among your people, something may already stand plain in one person's sight, waiting on what they will do with the seeing.  
> ✦ What small door has stood in that house all along, that one step of light would show you?

**sol 6.1**  
> In your family’s home, you see a single lamp above the bench, then only the worn edge beneath it. With Kenaz, you meet the fire of understanding, not certainty. You can see wear without knowing whose hands leave it. Among your people, closeness may coexist with something you no longer overlook.  
> ✦ What comes into view for you when clarity reaches only a little further?

## Ansuz × Healing & Wellbeing

### A — dnešní prompt: „nech obraz přistát na oblasti“

**sol 6 (tvoje čtení v appce)**  
> The bell stays fixed across the fjord while its sound thins over the water, and you turn toward it before you think to. Ansuz speaks to guidance you can hear without having to search for it. In the still air, even the last low note reaches you. Could hearing what comes to you now change what gives you strength?  
> ✦ Is there a word that reaches you at the right moment and stays?

**Opus 5 (testeři)**  
> Across the fjord a bell sounds, and the water, the air, the light all shift around it while the sound itself holds steady. You turn toward it before you have decided to turn. Ansuz is the rune of the word that arrives and asks to be heard, not read into. If something has been reaching you plainly, what might change in your strength when you stop translating it?  
> ✦ A word spoken once can stay with you for years — have you noticed which ones chose to stay?

**sol 6.1**  
> You face the bell tower across the fjord as its note fades in the still air. You turn toward the sound without deciding to, with your collar resting against your cheek. Ansuz names divine guidance received through words and listening. What might give you strength again if you hear without having to answer?  
> ✦ Which words stay with you longer than you expect?
