# Vzorky run, ke kterým Algiz utíká (cela A = nedotčená produkce, ident25)

## Algiz

**Běh 1** · soudce: → Isa (Isa/Isa/Isa)

> You stand in the doorway of the turf house, and the thick wall takes the wind so the air around you holds still. Algiz is that wall — the low ground where the work stays steady while the weather passes over. The looking for is done from here, not out in the gale. This is where you stand, Kuky, inside the calm the wall keeps.

**Běh 2** · soudce: → Thurisaz (Thurisaz/Thurisaz/Thurisaz)

> You stand in the doorway of the turf house while the wind runs hard along the wall outside. Algiz is that wall standing between the gale and the quiet where your hands can work. The old wall does not stop the wind, it only gives you a still place to make from. Here in the calm of the doorway you decide what leaves your hands, Kuky.

## Thurisaz

**Běh 1** · soudce: SPRÁVNĚ (Thurisaz/Thurisaz/Thurisaz)

> Under the far lip of the crack the old lava is cooler, already crusted, though the fire that split the rock still moves somewhere below. Thurisaz is the edge that will not let you walk across as if there were no gap. The ground you stand on holds your weight, and the ground beyond is close enough to see the moss on it. What are you asking of the far side that this side has stopped giving?

**Běh 2** · soudce: SPRÁVNĚ (Thurisaz/Thurisaz/Thurisaz)

> On the far side of the crack the ground looks the same as here, dark rock, low moss, no sign of what waits there. Thurisaz is the edge itself, the gap that will not be walked across but only jumped. You stand at the near rim, measuring the width with your eyes. What are you carrying that you would not want to land holding?

**Běh 2** · soudce: SPRÁVNĚ (Berkana/Berkana/Berkana)

> Where the barn door opens onto wet spring grass, the lambs stand where warmth ends and open ground begins. Berkana is that first step taken on legs that have never yet walked. The work you are made for is not behind the door but out in the field, and the ground holds. You are learning to stand where you have not stood, Kuky, and the standing comes.

This is Isa, Kuky, the stillness that holds a thing where it is until someone reaches for the key. Beneath the wish for new work sits the question of what has quietly gone unwound. The room is not waiting for the hour to turn on its own.

**Běh 2** · soudce: SPRÁVNĚ (Isa/Isa/Isa)

> The pendulum hangs still behind the glass, one small brass weight no one thinks to look at. Isa is the pause that holds a thing where it stands, Kuky, until a hand comes to move it. Beneath the wish for new work sits the quiet question of what stopped the hands here. The clock does not tell time it is not wound to keep.

**Běh 2** · soudce: SPRÁVNĚ (Othila/Othila/Othila)

# VERDIKT ČTENÁŘE (Cowork-tune, 2026-09-10) — mechanismus nalezen v textech
**Nota se neudržela — sklouzla.** Byla v zadání, ale **text jde za obrazem, ne za aspektem**,
a každý zvolený předmět seděl na cizím pozemku: kámen = nehybnost/dědictví (Isa/Othila) ·
čekání na počasí = Isa · práh/dveře = doslova Thurisazovo jádro · ovce+jehně = Berkanina
rodina · „care sent ahead" = Berkana. Potvrzeno i na PRODUKČNÍM obraze (turf-zeď utekla
k Ise a Thurisazu) → **strukturální, ne jeden špatný obraz.**
**Kde Algiz držel (recept, testovatelný soudcem):** ochranná přítomnost **aktivně vložená**
mezi tebe a **pojmenovanou** hrozbu; ochránce je **subjekt v popředí**; „vyšší/neviditelné"
jede jako vrstva (nevyžádaná, zdroj nevidíš) — ne jako abstraktní gesto; **vyhnout se
předmětům cizích run** (práh/dveře, ovce/jehně, zeď-co-ztiší, staré-pamatující věci, silnice).
Zkratka: **Algiz ≠ úkryt, ≠ bariéra. Algiz = někdo/něco se postaví mezi tebe a ránu, a tys
o to neprosil.**
