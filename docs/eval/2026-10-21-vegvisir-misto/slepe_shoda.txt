=== 1 ===
RUNE: Gebo — gift, companionship, giving and receiving, balance
PLACE: A forge inside a cave, the fire banked under ash.
READING: Gebo stands at the mouth of a cave, and inside it a forge sits low against the rock. The fire is banked under ash, grey on top, red beneath. Someone covered it last night so it would still be alive by morning. That is a small gift, given to whoever comes next. The bellows hang on a peg, the leather cracked where hands have held it. A pile of dry wood waits by the hearth. The ash is warm if you hold your palm above it. Nothing here has gone out.

=== 2 ===
RUNE: Gebo — gift, companionship, giving and receiving, balance
PLACE: A rope stretched between two banks of a river.
READING: Gebo crosses the water as a rope, stretched tight between two banks of a river. One end is tied to an alder, the other to a post driven deep into the far bank. Neither side could hold it alone. The river runs fast and brown beneath, and the rope hums a little when the wind catches it. Where hands have gripped it most, the fibres are worn smooth. Someone on each bank checks the knot from time to time. The rope stays taut because both ends keep pulling.

=== 3 ===
RUNE: Gebo — gift, companionship, giving and receiving, balance
PLACE: A forge inside a cave, the fire banked under ash.
READING: Gebo leads you into a cave where a forge stands against the back wall. The fire is banked under a thick layer of ash. It looks dead from the doorway, but the air near the hearth is warm. Whoever worked here last took the time to cover the coals, so the next hand would not need to start from flint. The anvil is cold and clean. A pair of tongs lies across it, left where someone could find them. The ash shifts a little, and a thin line of red shows through.

=== 4 ===
RUNE: Gebo — gift, companionship, giving and receiving, balance
PLACE: A rope stretched between two banks of a river.
READING: Gebo shows a rope stretched between two banks of a river. It is tied off at both ends, around a birch on this side and a boulder on the other. The water below is quick and cold, loud over stones. The rope sags a little in the middle, where the weight of every crossing has settled. Each end holds the other up. Cut one knot and the whole line falls into the current. A wet glove hangs from it halfway across, caught on a twist of hemp. The far bank is close enough to see clearly.

=== 5 ===
RUNE: Gebo — gift, companionship, giving and receiving, balance
PLACE: A forge inside a cave, the fire banked under ash.
READING: Gebo stands in a cave where a forge has been left for the night. The fire is banked under ash, the coals tucked down so they keep their heat until morning. Someone did that work for whoever comes after. Smoke drifts up thinly and finds a crack in the rock overhead. Iron bars lean against the wall, some finished, some still rough. A bucket of water sits by the anvil, its surface black and still. If you lift the ash with a stick, the red underneath answers the air.

=== 6 ===
RUNE: Gebo — gift, companionship, giving and receiving, balance
PLACE: A rope stretched between two banks of a river.
READING: Gebo is a rope stretched across a river, from one bank to the other. On this side it is lashed to a post, on the far side to an old rowan. The river pulls hard at anything that touches it. The rope holds because it is fastened twice, once at each end, and both knots are sound. Its hemp is darker where many hands have passed along it. Midway, it dips close to the water, and spray beads on the fibres. On the other bank a path climbs away between the stones.

=== 7 ===
RUNE: Gebo — gift, companionship, giving and receiving, balance
PLACE: A forge inside a cave, the fire banked under ash.
READING: Gebo opens a cave in the hillside, and at its back stands a forge. The fire is banked under ash, kept low so it lasts. Somebody laid that ash with care, a gift to the morning and to whoever works the iron next. The hammer rests on the anvil, its handle dark from use. Near the hearth the rock is warm to the touch, though the cave mouth lets in cold. A single coal glows where the ash is thinnest. The bellows are within reach.

=== 8 ===
RUNE: Gebo — gift, companionship, giving and receiving, balance
PLACE: A forge inside a cave, the fire banked under ash.
READING: Gebo brings you to a forge set deep inside a cave. The fire is banked under ash, quiet and grey, holding its heat the way a sleeping animal holds its warmth. Someone banked it on purpose before they left. The smith who comes next will not have to strike a spark. Along the wall hang tools in a neat row, tongs and punches and a worn file. Water drips somewhere further in, slow and steady. When a draught moves through the cave, the ash breathes a faint red.

=== 9 ===
RUNE: Gebo — gift, companionship, giving and receiving, balance
PLACE: A rope stretched between two banks of a river.
READING: Gebo stretches a rope between two banks of a river. It is tied to a stake on one shore and a thick willow on the other. Below it the water runs high with meltwater, grey and fast. The rope carries what neither bank can carry alone, a way across. It gives a little under weight and then holds. Someone has wrapped a strip of cloth around it where the knot rubs against bark. The river keeps moving underneath, and the rope stays where it is tied.

=== 10 ===
RUNE: Gebo — gift, companionship, giving and receiving, balance
PLACE: A rope stretched between two banks of a river.
READING: Gebo is a rope stretched tight across a river, bank to bank. On the near side it is bound round a stone cairn, on the far side round a wooden post. The current runs strong and pulls at the reeds along the edges. The rope does not touch the water. It holds because two ends hold it, and each takes half the strain. Its fibres are frayed in places and mended in others with newer cord. A bird lands on it, rides the small sway, and flies on.
