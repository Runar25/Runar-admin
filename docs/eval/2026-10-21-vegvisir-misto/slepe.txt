PLACE: A forge inside a cave, the fire banked under ash.
READING: Light comes in at the mouth of the cave, a long bar of it reaching across the floor toward the forge. It stops just short of the ash. The fire lies banked under grey, holding its heat low where the sun cannot touch it. Two kinds of warmth sit in this place, the one from outside and the one kept in here. The anvil stands between them, half in light and half in shadow. A spark lifts from the ash on some small draught, glows for a moment, and goes out.

PLACE: A rope stretched between two banks of a river.
READING: The rope runs from one bank to the other, tied off around two boulders. Rime has gathered along its length, a thin white crust that cracks when you touch it. Wind comes down the valley and the rope barely stirs, stiff with cold. On the far bank the boulder sits exactly where it sat, and the line between them is clear.
