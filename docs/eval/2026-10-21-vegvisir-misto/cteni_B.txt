=== 1 ===
You stand halfway across the footbridge, and the planks are rimed with frost. Below you the glacial river runs grey and loud, carrying silt down from the ice. The bank behind you is still close enough to show each stone. The far bank sits in mist, its outline not yet settled. Here the cold holds everything still for a moment. The rope rail is stiff under your hand, and your breath hangs in the air. Nothing on this bridge moves except the water. The question waits with you here, halfway, in the cold.
=== 2 ===
The cave is cold at the mouth, with ice along the rock where water once ran. Further in stands a forge, and its fire lies banked under grey ash. No flame shows, and no smith works the bellows. Yet the stones around the hearth hold a faint warmth, and the iron on the anvil has not rusted. Ash keeps the coals the way snow keeps the ground beneath it. Everything here rests in stillness. Hold your palm above the ash, and the heat rises slowly into it. The coals lie there, unseen, under the grey.
=== 3 ===
A rope stretches across the river, tied fast to a post on each bank. Frost has gathered along its length, and it hangs stiff in the cold air. The water beneath runs dark between thin shelves of ice. Nothing crosses the rope right now. Still it holds the two banks to each other, pulled taut from either side. The ice has stopped the sway it would have in summer. Each knot carries the strain of the other bank. Whatever travels along this rope travels slowly here, in the still air above the river.
=== 4 ===
You stand halfway across the footbridge, the glacial river loud and milky beneath the planks. Behind you, on the near bank, cattle graze the green slope, and a byre stands with its door open. Everything gathered there is easy to count. Ahead the far bank is bare gravel, with no fence and no field marked out. The bridge sways a little under your weight. Whatever you carry across has to fit in your hands. The river does not stop for anyone, it keeps grinding stone down to sand. The far bank lies open.
=== 5 ===
Inside the cave the forge sits quiet, its fire banked under a thick layer of ash. Along the wall hang tools worn smooth by years of hands, tongs and hammers each in their place. A few bars of iron lie stacked beside the anvil, enough for many days of work. The ash looks dead and grey, yet the coals beneath have kept their heat through the night. A smith banks a fire this way to come back to it. Wealth in this cave is held in heat and iron. Under the ash the coals still glow.
=== 6 ===
A rope stretches across the river from one bank to the other. On it hangs a small wooden carrier, the kind farmers use to send goods over when the water runs too high. Wool goes one way, salted fish comes back the other. The rope is worn smooth where it rubs the posts, polished by many loads. Each bank has something the other lacks. The carrier hangs halfway over the water now, swaying slightly in the wind. Whoever pulls next decides which bank it reaches. The rope stays taut between the two posts.
=== 7 ===
You stand halfway across the footbridge, and the sun has broken through above the glacier. Light runs along the river below, turning the grey water bright and hard to look at. Your shadow falls long across the planks, pointing back toward the bank you left. The near bank lies in plain light, every stone and path clear. The far bank shines too, but the glare blurs its edges into white. Warmth reaches the planks, and the frost on them begins to lift. The river keeps running beneath you, loud and bright in the sun.
=== 8 ===
The cave is dim, and the forge fire lies banked under grey ash. A single shaft of sun comes through a crack in the rock and falls across the hearth. In that light the ash looks pale and still. Yet heat rises from it, enough to bend the air above the coals. The sun outside and the fire within are the same kind of thing, one high and one buried. A breath of wind from the cave mouth stirs the ash, and for a moment a red edge shows. Then the grey settles over it again.
=== 9 ===
A rope stretches between two banks of a river, and the noon sun lies full on it. Its shadow crosses the water as a thin dark line. Someone on the far bank has hold of one end, you hold the other. When one of you pulls, the other feels it in the palms. The sun dries the rope as it hangs, and the fibres tighten. Light falls equally on both banks, with no side left in shade. Along the rope the warmth passes from hand to hand. The river runs on beneath it, bright and quick.
