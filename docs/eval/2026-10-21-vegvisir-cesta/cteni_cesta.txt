=== 1 ===
Mannaz stands in a turf house with its door left open to the morning. Grass grows thick on the roof, and the walls still hold the night's warmth. Light comes in across the packed earth floor, as far as the hearth. Someone lived here long enough to wear a hollow into the threshold stone. A bench runs along the wall, and a cup rests on it, half full. Outside, a sheep crops the grass close to the door. Cold air and warm air meet in the doorway, and neither one gives way.

=== 2 ===
Ehwaz finds you halfway across a narrow footbridge above a glacial river. The water below runs grey with ground stone, loud enough to drown a voice. The planks are worn pale in the middle, where many feet have gone before yours. Each step sets the bridge swaying, and the sway answers back a moment later. The rope rail is wet and cold under your hand. Behind you the bank is as far as the bank ahead. The river does not slow for anyone, and the bridge holds the weight it is given.

=== 3 ===
The blank stone comes to you in a lava cave, where meltwater drips into a dark pool. The rock overhead was once molten, and it still holds the ripples of its flowing. A drop falls, and the sound carries a long way into the dark. Rings spread across the pool and fade before they reach the edge. You cannot see the bottom, and the surface gives back nothing. The air is cold and smells of wet stone. Between one drop and the next, there is only the stillness of the water.

=== 4 ===
Kenaz shows a rope stretched between two banks of a river. It is tied to a post on one side and a rock on the other, knotted by someone who knew knots. The middle sags low, close enough to the current that spray darkens the fibres. Where hands have gripped it, the rope is smooth and almost shining. The water pulls at it without pause, and the rope hums faintly under the strain. On the far bank a path is worn into the grass. Someone crossed here, and the rope still shows where they held on.

=== 5 ===
Isa lies in a forge inside a cave, the fire banked under ash. The bellows hang still, and the anvil is cold to the touch. Under the grey layer, a few coals keep a dull red, giving off a slow heat. The smith's tools rest on their pegs in the order they were left. Nothing is being shaped here now. Water stands in the quenching trough without a ripple. The cave keeps the cold out and the warmth in, and the fire waits the way stone waits, without hurry.

=== 6 ===
Sowilo comes to a mountain pass wrapped in fog, with a cairn standing at the top. The path climbs over loose scree, and the stones shift a little underfoot. Ahead there is only grey, and behind there is only grey. Then the cairn takes shape, stone set on stone by many hands over many years. Each traveller who crossed added one, and the pile has grown taller than a man. Above the fog, the sun is already on the ridge. Its light thins the grey from the top down, slowly.

=== 7 ===
Ingwaz rests in a boat pulled up on black sand, two oars laid inside it. The hull is tarred dark, and the keel has cut a long groove in the beach. The tide has drawn back and left the sand wet and shining. Inside, the oars lie side by side, their blades still damp. A coil of rope sits in the bow, and a bailing scoop rolls gently against the ribs. Waves break further out and slide in thin across the sand. The sea comes a little closer with each one, and the boat lies still.

=== 8 ===
Fehu meets you at a hot spring steaming in the snow at dusk. The water rises warm from deep in the ground, and it has done so for longer than anyone has kept count. Snow lies right to the edge, yet the stones around the pool are bare and dark. Steam lifts and drifts across the last of the light. A few horses stand nearby, their breath clouding, their backs dusted white. The cold presses in from every side, and the water keeps giving its heat to anyone who steps in.
