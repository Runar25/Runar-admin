# Round 4: candidate images, everyday and from nature (lab, 2026-11-16)

For each rune: the mechanism in one sentence, the tension (in Czech), and the candidate images. Nothing here is in production.

## Fehu
Mechanism: Value that lives only while it moves: what is used and passed on grows, what is left standing loses its strength.
Napětí: Hodnota, kterou máš, žije jen pohybem: co se používá a jde dál, roste; co stojí, ztrácí sílu.
1. The candle kept for a special evening has never been lit.
2. The battery you never use is flat on the day you need it.
3. The pool cut off from the river clouds over while the river runs clear.
4. The bucket under the spring is full, and the water goes on running over its brim.
5. After weeks on the sofa, the stiff knee meets the stairs.
6. The breath has to go out before the next one can come in.

## Uruz
Mechanism: Raw bodily strength that shows only when it is needed: a weight or resistance gives way to a force that was already there.
Napětí: Síla, o které nevíš, dokud ji nepotřebuješ: něco, co vypadalo nepohnutelně, povolí.
7. The lid holds, and holds, and then turns in your hand.
8. The salmon throws itself at the falls again.
9. The door swollen by rain opens at the third push of your shoulder.

## Thurisaz
Mechanism: The sharp edge at a threshold: something stops a move just before it is made, and the stop protects or shows what the rush would have missed.
Napětí: Okamžik mezi impulsem a činem: něco ostrého tě zastaví těsně před krokem — a právě to zastavení chrání.
10. The reply is written in a single breath, and your thumb stops above the send button.
11. The cold of the river at your ankles stops you before the current does.
12. Your foot is already off the kerb when the horn sounds.
13. The answer is already on your tongue, and you take one long breath before it.

## Ansuz
Mechanism: A word or sound that arrives and changes something: speech heard at the right moment gives a thing its name or its meaning.
Napětí: Slovo, které už možná zaznělo mnohokrát, a teprve teď je slyšet — a tím se něco změní.
14. One person says the name no one has said all evening, and the table goes quiet.
15. Said aloud, the plan sounds different from the one in your head.
16. The raven calls once from the roof, and everyone at the door looks up.

## Raidho
Mechanism: A road made by going: step follows step, a rhythm is found only by moving, and the question is whether the way still leads where one means to go.
Napětí: Cestu dělají kroky, ne plán — a snadná cesta tě může nést dál, než kam jsi chtěl.
17. The path across the grass is on no plan; it is where everyone actually walks.
18. On the long drive you miss your turning because the road was so easy to follow.
19. After the first hour of walking your feet stop asking how far it is.

## Kenaz
Mechanism: A small light that reveals: something that was there all along gets a shape, and once seen it cannot be unseen; a flame that needs feeding.
Napětí: Malé světlo ukáže, co tu bylo celou dobu — i to, co bys raději neviděl; a oheň bez péče zhasne.
20. The phone light finds the dropped key, and beside it a long-lost earring.
21. The new bulb in the hallway shows a crack that was always in the ceiling.
22. One match in the cave shows the wall an arm's length away and no further.

## Gebo
Mechanism: A gift between two sides: something leaves one hand and is received by another, and what arises between them belongs to both and is not counted.
Napětí: Dar, který nikdo nepočítá, a přece mezi dvěma něco založí: obě strany dávají i přijímají.
23. You bring a dish to the shared dinner and leave with a plate of someone else's cooking.
24. The borrowed book comes back with a bar of chocolate on top.
25. Someone has remembered how you take your coffee.
26. The rowan feeds the thrush, and the thrush carries its seed over the hill.
27. A gift you did not ask for sits on your table, still in its paper.
28. The plank across the stream holds because each bank carries one end.

## Wunjo
Mechanism: A moment when things fall into place and lighten: shared joy in the middle of things, not a reward for finishing.
Napětí: Nic se nevyřešilo, a přece je to lehčí: radost uprostřed práce nebo nepohody, ne až po ní.
29. Halfway through the dishes a song comes on, and you both join in.
30. Dinner is burnt, and the table laughs over bread and cheese.
31. On the first warm day the doors along the street stand open.

## Hagalaz
Mechanism: An uninvited force from outside breaks what looked solid: the plan falls apart, and afterwards it shows what is gone and what held.
Napětí: Co nikdo nezval, rozbije, co vypadalo pevně — a teprve potom je vidět, co zbylo.
32. The flight is cancelled at the gate, and the queue turns round as one.
33. A power cut ends the video call and takes the unsaved slide with it.
34. One minute of hail, and the tulips are lying on the path.

## Nauthiz
Mechanism: Lack and constraint: something is missing or cannot be had, and the pressure shows what is truly needed.
Napětí: Teprve když něčeho není dost, je vidět, co je opravdu potřeba.
35. The phone is at two per cent, and you send the one message that matters.
36. One seat is left on the last bus, and two of you are still on the pavement.
37. The birch on the lava field grows along the one crack that holds soil.

## Isa
Mechanism: Standstill: something that was moving has stopped and is held as it is, and in the stillness one thing stays in view.
Napětí: Owner 2026-11-14: něco, co by se normálně pohybovalo, je teď zastavené — a to zastavení má konkrétní důsledek.
38. The waterfall has become a wall of ice, its last streams held against the rock.
39. A chain of bubbles rises through the dark water, then stops beneath the clear ice.
40. A drop has frozen at the tip of an icicle, just before it falls.
41. The lift stops between floors, and the building goes on humming around it.

## Jera
Mechanism: Time that does the work: what was done long before ripens at its own pace and shows as a result that cannot be hurried.
Napětí: Dlouho není nic vidět a nejde to uspíšit — a pak se ukáže výsledek toho, co bylo uděláno dávno.
42. The tree planted as a sapling beside the bench now shades the place where you sit.
43. After months of practice, you answer in the new language without translating first.
44. The coins dropped in the jar each evening are, one day, the fare.

## Eihwaz
Mechanism: Holding through change: something bends, stands between two states or endures a long strain, and does not break.
Napětí: Všechno kolem se mění nebo tlačí, a jedna věc drží — ohne se, ale nezlomí.
45. The footbridge sways under the crowd and is still again when they have crossed.
46. The tall grass lies flat in the wind and is standing again by evening.
47. The runner slows to a walk on the hill and does not stop.

## Perth
Mechanism: The closed vessel: something exists but is not yet shown or known — not "nothing", but "not yet".
Napětí: Obsah existuje, ale je zavřený: ne „nic nevím“, ale „ještě nevím“ — a otevřít to dřív by to pokazilo.
48. A parcel sits on the step, taped shut, with no sender's name.
49. An unnamed voice memo sits in your phone, three minutes long.
50. The whale has dived, and everyone on the boat watches a different patch of water.
51. You write down the teacher's words, not yet knowing what they are for.

## Algiz
Mechanism: Guarding alertness: something watches or stands between a threat and what it protects, open to the world yet letting not everything in.
Napětí: Otevřené světu, a přece hlídá: pustí dovnitř jen něco — a tím to, co je uvnitř, ochrání.
52. The door opens on its chain, just far enough to see who is knocking.
53. The phone is silenced for the night, with three numbers allowed to ring through.
54. The flock feeds head down, and one goose stands with its neck straight.

## Sowilo
Mechanism: The sun's light and strength: it simply shines, and by it one sees where one stands; too bright, it dazzles.
Napětí: Slunce nic nevysvětluje, jen svítí — a najednou je vidět, kde stojíš; příliš ostré oslní.
55. The cloud lifts, and you see how far up the slope you already are.
56. The low sun comes in under the cloud, and the stones on the moor have shadows.
57. The sun comes round to the window, and every fingerprint on the glass shows.

## Tiwaz
Mechanism: A direction that costs something: taking one side or keeping one's word and paying the price for it knowingly.
Napětí: Nejde stát na všech stranách: jeden směr vybereš a víš, co tě to stojí.
58. Everyone agreed in the corridor; on the form there is one name, and it is yours.
59. You tell the referee the ball was out, and your own team loses the point.
60. You turn down the praise and say whose work it was.

## Berkana
Mechanism: New small life and the care that lets it grow: something fragile begins, and is given room rather than forced.
Napětí: Malé není slabé — a péče znamená dát tomu místo, ne do toho sahat.
61. The child struggles with the zip, and you wait while they try again.
62. The seedling droops for a week in its new pot, then puts out one leaf.
63. The eider plucks her own down to line the nest.

## Ehwaz
Mechanism: Movement made by two: progress that comes only when two find a common rhythm and neither forces the other.
Napětí: Pohyb vznikne až ve chvíli, kdy ani jeden netlačí: dva najdou společný krok.
64. Two people walk side by side, and neither could say who set the pace.
65. Two people carry a long mirror through the doorway, one pausing as the other turns.
66. Two swans cross the lake, and the gap between them stays the same.

## Mannaz
Mechanism: The person among people: one sees oneself in or through others, as in a mirror.
Napětí: Sám sebe vidíš až v druhých: oni ukážou to, co zevnitř vidět nejde.
67. You hear your own phrase come out of a friend's mouth and learn how it sounds.
68. A friend copies the way you tap your foot, and you had never noticed it.
69. In the dark window of the train your own face sits among the other passengers.

## Laguz
Mechanism: Water and what moves under the surface: something is sensed before it can be explained, or a hidden current finds its own way.
Napětí: Víš to dřív, než to umíš vysvětlit: pod klidnou hladinou se něco hýbe a určuje směr.
70. Your foot finds the brake as the car ahead drifts, before you know why.
71. You turn back at the door, sure something is left behind, before you know what.
72. The swell lifts the boat before any wave can be seen.

## Ingwaz
Mechanism: Ripening inside: from outside nothing seems to happen while something completes itself within, closed until it is ready.
Napětí: Zvenku se neděje nic, uvnitř všechno: je to zavřené a hotové to bude, až to bude hotové.
73. The dough rests under a tea towel, and by morning it has doubled.
74. The broken bone knits under the cast, and nothing can be seen of it.
75. The potato forgotten in the cupboard has put out white shoots.

## Othila
Mechanism: What is inherited and where one returns: things, ways and places handed down, and the choice of what to carry on.
Napětí: Co jsi dostal, a co z toho neseš dál: něco zděděného pořád slouží, něco už nepasuje.
76. The recipe card is in another hand, with one change added in yours.
77. The table has moved through three homes and still stands in the kitchen.
78. The stream runs in the bed the old river cut.

## Dagaz
Mechanism: The turn from dark to light: a shift after which the same world looks different and cannot be seen the old way.
Napětí: Nic se nepohnulo, a přece je všechno jinak: pohled se otočí a zpátky už to nejde.
79. You read the same message again in the morning, and it says something else.
80. You turn the map on your phone, and the street falls into place.
81. The tide has turned, and the same boats now point the other way.

## Blank
Mechanism: The unmarked: no sign, no name yet — an open space where meaning has not been written.
Napětí: Prázdné místo není chyba: znak tu ještě není, a co se tam vloží moc rychle, zakryje, co se teprve ukazuje.
82. A new document is open, and the cursor blinks on the first line.
83. One wall in the new flat is still bare, without a single nail hole.
84. Fresh snow lies on the field, and nothing has crossed it yet.
