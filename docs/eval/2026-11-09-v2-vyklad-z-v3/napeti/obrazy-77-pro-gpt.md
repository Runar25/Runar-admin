# 77 candidate images for 25 runes (lab, 2026-11-14)

For each rune: the mechanism in one sentence, the tension (in Czech), and the candidate images. Nothing here is in production.

## Fehu
Mechanism: Value that lives only while it moves: what is used and passed on grows, what is left standing loses its strength.
Napětí: Hodnota, kterou máš, žije jen pohybem: co se používá a jde dál, roste; co stojí, ztrácí sílu.
1. The rain barrel fills to the brim and runs over into the bed where the potatoes grow.
2. The battery you never use is flat on the day you need it.
3. The bicycle ridden every day runs smoothly; the one kept in the shed has seized.

## Uruz
Mechanism: Raw bodily strength that shows only when it is needed: a weight or resistance gives way to a force that was already there.
Napětí: Síla, o které nevíš, dokud ji nepotřebuješ: něco, co vypadalo nepohnutelně, povolí.
4. The car sits fast in the snow until you put your shoulder to it, and it rolls.
5. The jammed window gives all at once when you put your whole weight behind it.
6. A root has lifted the paving stone and tipped it out of the path.

## Thurisaz
Mechanism: The sharp edge at a threshold: something stops a move just before it is made, and the stop protects or shows what the rush would have missed.
Napětí: Okamžik mezi impulsem a činem: něco ostrého tě zastaví těsně před krokem — a právě to zastavení chrání.
7. You have typed the angry reply, and your thumb stops above the send button.
8. The cat hisses once, and your hand stops short of her kittens.
9. The red light holds you at the crossing while a lorry goes through.

## Ansuz
Mechanism: A word or sound that arrives and changes something: speech heard at the right moment gives a thing its name or its meaning.
Napětí: Slovo, které už možná zaznělo mnohokrát, a teprve teď je slyšet — a tím se něco změní.
10. Someone says aloud what the whole table was thinking, and the room breathes out.
11. The advice you had heard many times makes sense the day you say it to someone else.
12. The radio has been on all day, and you hear it only when it says the name of your street.

## Raidho
Mechanism: A road made by going: step follows step, a rhythm is found only by moving, and the question is whether the way still leads where one means to go.
Napětí: Cestu dělají kroky, ne plán — a snadná cesta tě může nést dál, než kam jsi chtěl.
13. The path across the grass is on no plan; it is where everyone actually walks.
14. On the long drive you miss your turning because the road was so easy to follow.
15. The first lengths in the pool are a fight; then the arms find their own count.

## Kenaz
Mechanism: A small light that reveals: something that was there all along gets a shape, and once seen it cannot be unseen; a flame that needs feeding.
Napětí: Malé světlo ukáže, co tu bylo celou dobu — i to, co bys raději neviděl; a oheň bez péče zhasne.
16. The phone torch finds the keys under the car seat, and everything else that fell there.
17. You change the dead bulb in the hallway and see how long the walls have needed paint.
18. The idea comes in the shower, and by the time you find a pen half of it is gone.

## Gebo
Mechanism: A gift between two sides: something leaves one hand and is received by another, and what arises between them belongs to both and is not counted.
Napětí: Dar, který nikdo nepočítá, a přece mezi dvěma něco založí: obě strany dávají i přijímají.
19. The borrowed dish comes back with something baked in it.
20. At the table one pours the coffee and the other cuts the cake, and no one keeps count.
21. You hold out a hand to help someone up, and their grip steadies you too.
22. The bridge stands only because both banks carry it.

## Wunjo
Mechanism: A moment when things fall into place and lighten: shared joy in the middle of things, not a reward for finishing.
Napětí: Nic se nevyřešilo, a přece je to lehčí: radost uprostřed práce nebo nepohody, ne až po ní.
23. Halfway through the washing-up the song comes on, and both of you start to sing.
24. The rain keeps falling on the picnic, and the laughing under the one umbrella gets louder.
25. One piece of the puzzle is still missing, and everyone at the table is pleased with it anyway.

## Hagalaz
Mechanism: An uninvited force from outside breaks what looked solid: the plan falls apart, and afterwards it shows what is gone and what held.
Napětí: Co nikdo nezval, rozbije, co vypadalo pevně — a teprve potom je vidět, co zbylo.
26. The flight is cancelled at the gate, and the whole plan is gone with one announcement.
27. Hail drums on the car roof, and when it stops every leaf in the garden is on the ground.
28. The storm takes the trampoline over the fence and leaves the old shed standing.

## Nauthiz
Mechanism: Lack and constraint: something is missing or cannot be had, and the pressure shows what is truly needed.
Napětí: Teprve když něčeho není dost, je vidět, co je opravdu potřeba.
29. The phone is at two per cent, and you send the one message that matters.
30. With one match left, you build the fire properly before you strike it.
31. The water is off, and you notice every time your hand goes to the tap.

## Isa
Mechanism: Standstill: something that was moving has stopped and is held as it is, and in the stillness one thing stays in view.
Napětí: Owner 2026-11-14: něco, co by se normálně pohybovalo, je teď zastavené — a to zastavení má konkrétní důsledek.
32. The waterfall has become a wall of ice, its last streams held against the rock.
33. A chain of bubbles rises through the dark water, then stops beneath the clear ice.
34. A drop has frozen at the tip of an icicle, just before it falls.
35. The traffic has stopped on the mountain road, and people get out and look at the view.

## Jera
Mechanism: Time that does the work: what was done long before ripens at its own pace and shows as a result that cannot be hurried.
Napětí: Dlouho není nic vidět a nejde to uspíšit — a pak se ukáže výsledek toho, co bylo uděláno dávno.
36. You dig up the seed to see if it is growing, and now it is not.
37. The tree that went into the ground as a stick now shades the bench.
38. The language practised a little each day comes out of your mouth on its own.

## Eihwaz
Mechanism: Holding through change: something bends, stands between two states or endures a long strain, and does not break.
Napětí: Všechno kolem se mění nebo tlačí, a jedna věc drží — ohne se, ale nezlomí.
39. The tent pole bends double in the gust, and the tent is still there in the morning.
40. The house is stripped to its beams for rebuilding, and the old beams carry the new roof.
41. The milk tooth is gone, the gap is tender, and the new tooth has not come through.

## Perth
Mechanism: The closed vessel: something exists but is not yet shown or known — not "nothing", but "not yet".
Napětí: Obsah existuje, ale je zavřený: ne „nic nevím“, ale „ještě nevím“ — a otevřít to dřív by to pokazilo.
42. The parcel has no sender's name, and you turn it over once more before opening it.
43. The film is still in the old camera, and no one remembers what was photographed.
44. The oven door stays shut, because opening it now would let the cake fall.

## Algiz
Mechanism: Guarding alertness: something watches or stands between a threat and what it protects, open to the world yet letting not everything in.
Napětí: Otevřené světu, a přece hlídá: pustí dovnitř jen něco — a tím to, co je uvnitř, ochrání.
45. The door opens on its chain, just far enough to see who is knocking.
46. The lifeguard's whistle turns the swimmers back before they reach the current.
47. The bird drops low and drags a wing, and the fox follows her away from the nest.

## Sowilo
Mechanism: The sun's light and strength: it simply shines, and by it one sees where one stands; too bright, it dazzles.
Napětí: Slunce nic nevysvětluje, jen svítí — a najednou je vidět, kde stojíš; příliš ostré oslní.
48. The fog burns off by noon, and the whole valley you were walking through is suddenly there.
49. The sun comes round the house, and the frost on that side of the path is gone.
50. You come out of the cinema into full daylight and stand still until you can see.

## Tiwaz
Mechanism: A direction that costs something: taking one side or keeping one's word and paying the price for it knowingly.
Napětí: Nejde stát na všech stranách: jeden směr vybereš a víš, co tě to stojí.
51. You tell the referee the ball was out, and your own team loses the point.
52. The two invitations are for the same evening, and you answer one of them.
53. You sign your name under the complaint everyone else agreed with in the corridor.

## Berkana
Mechanism: New small life and the care that lets it grow: something fragile begins, and is given room rather than forced.
Napětí: Malé není slabé — a péče znamená dát tomu místo, ne do toho sahat.
54. A green shoot has split the tarmac at the edge of the car park.
55. The child ties the shoelace wrong, and the hand that could fix it stays in the pocket.
56. The seedling moved to a bigger pot droops at first and then puts out a new leaf.

## Ehwaz
Mechanism: Movement made by two: progress that comes only when two find a common rhythm and neither forces the other.
Napětí: Pohyb vznikne až ve chvíli, kdy ani jeden netlačí: dva najdou společný krok.
57. On the tandem the pedals fight until both riders find the same push, and then it flies.
58. The dog stops pulling when the lead goes slack, and the two of you walk on at one pace.
59. Two saw the log together, and the blade runs only when neither of them pushes.

## Mannaz
Mechanism: The person among people: one sees oneself in or through others, as in a mirror.
Napětí: Sám sebe vidíš až v druhých: oni ukážou to, co zevnitř vidět nejde.
60. You hear your own phrase come out of a friend's mouth and learn how it sounds.
61. In the group photo you look first for your own face.
62. Someone imitates the way you walk, and the whole room knows who it is.

## Laguz
Mechanism: Water and what moves under the surface: something is sensed before it can be explained, or a hidden current finds its own way.
Napětí: Víš to dřív, než to umíš vysvětlit: pod klidnou hladinou se něco hýbe a určuje směr.
63. Your foot finds the brake before you have seen why.
64. The float lies still on the water, then dips once before anything is seen.
65. The damp patch on the ceiling shows where the water has been travelling inside the wall.

## Ingwaz
Mechanism: Ripening inside: from outside nothing seems to happen while something completes itself within, closed until it is ready.
Napětí: Zvenku se neděje nic, uvnitř všechno: je to zavřené a hotové to bude, až to bude hotové.
66. The chrysalis hangs on the fence and does not move, and inside nothing is left of the caterpillar.
67. The suitcase stands packed by the door, and there is nothing more to put in.
68. The pear on the sill is still hard, and pressing it every day does not ripen it.

## Othila
Mechanism: What is inherited and where one returns: things, ways and places handed down, and the choice of what to carry on.
Napětí: Co jsi dostal, a co z toho neseš dál: něco zděděného pořád slouží, něco už nepasuje.
69. The recipe is followed from a card in handwriting that is not yours.
70. The old house key still hangs on the ring, though the lock was changed.
71. The swallows build again under the same eave, on what is left of the old nest.

## Dagaz
Mechanism: The turn from dark to light: a shift after which the same world looks different and cannot be seen the old way.
Napětí: Nic se nepohnulo, a přece je všechno jinak: pohled se otočí a zpátky už to nejde.
72. You turn the map the other way up, and the road you could not find is where it always was.
73. The picture that was a vase is suddenly two faces, and it will not go back.
74. Halfway through the tunnel the light ahead becomes larger than the light behind.

## Blank
Mechanism: The unmarked: no sign, no name yet — an open space where meaning has not been written.
Napětí: Prázdné místo není chyba: znak tu ještě není, a co se tam vloží moc rychle, zakryje, co se teprve ukazuje.
75. The cursor blinks at the top of an empty page, and nothing has been typed.
76. The form has a box with no label, and you leave it empty.
77. The signpost at the crossroads has lost its boards; only the post is left.
