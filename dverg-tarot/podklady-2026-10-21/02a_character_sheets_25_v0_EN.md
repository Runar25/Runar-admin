Method: brief §4.1 — a **Character Sheet** holds what stays fixed about the dwarf; a **Rune Scene** holds what varies. One dwarf can then appear in several scenes.

## How to read

- Everything here is **[naše]** design specification unless a line says otherwise. Origin of the *name* is tagged per brief §4.4 and rated in `01_criteria_table_25x5_v0_EN.md`.
- **⊕ = a detail not in v0** that I added so the sheet is complete (age, hair, beard, signature item). It is a proposal, not a finding. Everything without ⊕ comes from v0.
- **Own rune** means the glyph on the pendant. The forehead mark is handled in `04_family_minimum_v0_EN.md`; where a sheet says "forehead: see 04" it is not a settled feature.
- **Palette** uses only the seven Agndofa colours (`sacred-palette.png`: whisper blue #5BB8FF · obsidian #1E1E1E · ember gold #D6A85C · rune cloth blue #2E4A70 · aurora green #7ED9B5 · frost white #F2F5F7 · hearthwood #7D5838). Four v0 colours are not in it — changed and marked **(palette ⊕)**.
- Names are working names unless the origin says otherwise; ON/IS forms are unchecked. Hair/beard "rings, cuffs" refer to the metal cuffs visible on Rúnar's braids in `runar-fullbody.png`.
- **Must not change** = what a generator must keep when the dwarf is drawn again in another scene. **Never** = what the picture of this character must not become (from v0).

Fields per sheet: **Origin · Personality · Craft/purpose · Place in Agndofa · Build & face ⊕ · Hair & beard · Clothing · Palette · Own rune · Differs from Rúnar and the others · Must not change · Never.**

## Cross-sheet checks

- **Colour overlap with Rúnar (blue coat):** Alvíss, Dvalin, Norðri, Perth, Tiwaz, Dagaz are all blue/grey-blue dominants. Rúnar is identified by his rune-cloth-blue coat. These six should lean pale/white-grey (Alvíss, Dvalin, Norðri), indigo-black (Perth), steel (Tiwaz) or two-colour (Dagaz) — **none in saturated rune-cloth blue**. Marked in the sheets' palette lines.
