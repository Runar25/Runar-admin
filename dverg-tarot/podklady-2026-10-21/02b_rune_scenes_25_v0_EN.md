## How to read

**Each scene lists:** situation and the verb the dwarf performs · meaning → element map · layers · the question the image leaves open · what the image must not claim or predict · the prompt's variable part.

- **Meaning → element map.** Meanings come from the owner's rune descriptions (`RUNAR_POPISY_RUN.md`, "[POP]") and the keywords in `v2/runar-runes.js` ("[K]"). **"— none yet" marks a meaning that the v0 scene does not carry.** That is the audit the brief's rule "1 meaning = 1 element" asks for; it is not a request to add elements (more than five or six and the figure disappears).
- **Question that stays open** is a short gloss of the closing question in [POP] — a question for the *viewer*, never advice. It is context for the scene, not text on the card.
- **v1 change** = where I propose to depart from v0 and why. Each change is a proposal for the owner; the v0 prompt is kept next to it.
- **Prompts** are the variable part only. The shared style lock (v0 §B) goes in front; I propose changes to it in `04_family_minimum_v0_EN.md`. The pendant carries the dwarf's own rune; the forehead mark is **not** in these prompts (see 04).
- **Applies to every card:** no text or letters in the image except the glowing rune; the lower fifth stays calm; Icelandic nature, with the markers the generator must be given (black lava rock through snow, moss-covered lava fields, a fjord with steep bare mountains, low birch scrub, turf houses, low winter sun, ice blocks with black ash stripes; no conifers, deer, sleds, igloos, polar bears — from `ISA_scene_study_v0_EN.md` §4); **no card says what will happen** ([POP]: "Ani jeden popis neříká, co se stane; všechny říkají, na co se dívat").
- Scenes for **Uruz, Gebo, Thurisaz, Dagaz** are written out with 2–3 angles in `03_scenes_*`; here they appear in short form.
- Scene for **Isa** is the finished study `ISA_scene_study_v0_EN.md`; here only its map is audited.

### 01 · Fehu — Straumr
- **Situation & verb:** at dusk by a glacial stream, he **lets flow** — offers water with one hand, the other open and empty.
- **Meaning → element:** value that moves → a scoop of running water · giving through the hands → the open hand held out · living value that grows when fed → sheep drifting into a fold · what stands still loses strength → **none yet**.
- **Layers:** *fore* scoop and two hands · *mid* stream · *back* sheep and fold, glacier, evening light.
- **Question left open:** what do you hold that could come alive if you let it flow?
- **Must not claim:** that wealth will come (no coins, no heaped goods).
- **Prompt:** *Straumr, ember-gold coat, standing at a glacial stream at golden evening, offering a wooden scoop of running water with one open hand, the other palm open and empty; sheep moving toward a stone fold behind him; obsidian pendant with ᚠ in whisper-blue light.*
- **v1 change:** none. Note: water also on Laguz (#21) — Fehu's water is **moving and carried**, Laguz's is **still and deep**.

### 04 · Ansuz — Alvíss
- **Situation & verb:** on the eastern heath at first light, he **listens**, one hand cupped behind the ear, mouth closed.
- **Meaning → element:** listening → cupped ear · word, voice and breath → **none yet** (in v0 nothing sounds) · message arriving → **none yet** · the word that covers (obscures) → **none yet**. v0's stone-pale edge of the cloak carries no meaning of Ansuz in [POP]; it is Alvíss's story.
- **Layers:** *fore* ear and hand · *mid* the dwarf · *back* pale heath, first light.
- **Question left open:** what changes when you truly hear what is coming to you?
- **Must not claim:** that talking is punished (the stone ending reads as a verdict; see file 01 #4).
- **v0 prompt:** *Alvíss, dwarf in a cool grey-blue coat, one hand cupped to his ear, listening, the edge of his cloak turning to pale stone in the first light on an eastern heath; obsidian pendant with ᚨ.*
- **v1 proposal:** drop the stone edge; add **a small bird singing on the rock beside him, beak open** (the sound arriving; his own mouth stays closed). Gives ear = listening, bird = the word/message, first light = right moment; three elements, one for each of Ansuz's meanings the card can show.
  *Prompt v1:* *Alvíss, dwarf in a cool white-grey coat with pale trim, one hand cupped to his ear, mouth closed, listening; a small bird sings on a rock beside him; pale eastern heath in first light; obsidian pendant with ᚨ.*
  *Argument against v1:* it loses the one detail unique to Alvíss's myth, and [VOL]/[ALV] give no support for a bird; the stone edge is also a handsome, distinctive image. Keep v0 if the owner values the myth over the legibility of "word".
